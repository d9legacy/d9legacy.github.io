The Supreme Council

Leadership

The real Omega Psi Phi is run by a team of elected brothers called the Supreme Council - think of them as the captains of the whole ship.
How It Works

Who Steers the Ship?

The Supreme Council is Omega’s board of directors: 24 brothers elected to lead. There are 9 Grand Officers (the top jobs), 3 Undergraduate Representatives (college students who speak up for students), and 12 District Representatives (one for each region of the world). The very biggest decisions happen at the Grand Conclave, Omega’s giant convention where brothers from everywhere gather to vote, learn and celebrate.

The 12 Districts

Omega Around the World

Omega divides the whole world into 12 districts, and each one elects a District Representative. The 1st District covers New England. The 9th covers Arkansas, Louisiana, Oklahoma and Texas. The 13th covers everything overseas - Jamaica, Japan, Ghana, England, the Bahamas and more. And here at D9Legacy, our favorite is the mighty 7th District: Alabama, Florida, Georgia and Mississippi - our owner Josh’s home turf.
