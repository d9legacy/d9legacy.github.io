About Omega

Who We Are

The first fraternity founded at a historically Black college - and still lifting as we climb, 115 years later.
Our Name

Friendship Is Essential to the Soul

Omega Psi Phi got its name from the initials of a Greek phrase that means “friendship is essential to the soul.” The founders liked it so much they made it the motto. In plain words: friends aren’t just nice to have - everybody needs them, deep down. That’s the whole idea of Omega.

People also say “Lifting as we climb.” It means when you do well, you reach back and pull somebody else up too. Nobody climbs alone.

The Four Cardinal Principles

What Every Omega Lives By

Manhood

Being a person of good character: brave, honest, and someone others can count on. Keep your word and do the right thing, even when it’s hard.

Scholarship

Loving to learn and taking school seriously. Omegas believe your brain is your superpower, so feed it every day.

Perseverance

Not quitting when things get tough. Stick with your team, stay humble, and go the extra mile for the goal.

Uplift

Sharing your gifts with your community through real help, leadership, and service. Lift as you climb.

Quick Facts

Omega at a Glance

  • FoundedNovember 17, 1911 at Howard University in Washington, D.C. - the first fraternity ever started at a historically Black college.
  • ColorsRoyal purple and old gold. You can see them all over this website.
  • NicknamesMembers are called Omega Men or Ques (rhymes with “keys”).
  • LettersΩΨΦ - the last letter of the Greek alphabet, Psi, and Phi. Omega means the grand finish of a journey.
  • FamilyOne of the Divine Nine, the nine historic Black Greek-letter organizations. See them all at D9Legacy.
  • EverywhereChapters across America and around the world - from Jamaica to Japan to Ghana.
Our Mission

Why Omega Exists

Omega brings together college men with big hearts and high goals, and turns them into leaders who serve. Every chapter works on the same mission: celebrate achievement, fund scholarships, mentor young people, register voters, fight for health, and lift whole communities. The real Omega Psi Phi calls these its mandated programs - and in the D9Legacy universe, we do the same.
