The Golden Rule of Omega

Zero Hazing. Ever.

The real Omega Psi Phi has a zero-tolerance rule against hazing. Hazing means anything done to someone joining that hurts, scares, embarrasses or wears them out - paddling, forced stunts, mean games, all-nighters, any of it. It is banned before, during and after the joining process, no exceptions.

If anyone ever treats you that way: tell a parent, teacher or trusted adult right away, and if it’s an emergency call 911. The real fraternity even runs a 24-hour hotline, 1-888-NOT-HAZE, and promises your safety comes first. Real brothers build you up - they never break you down.
