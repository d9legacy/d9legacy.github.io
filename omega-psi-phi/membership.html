The Real Path (For Grown-Ups)

How College Students Join

The real fraternity picks members carefully - it’s called the Membership Selection Process, and it’s completely voluntary. Here’s what a college student needs:

  • Step 1Be a full-time student at a four-year college in good standing.
  • Step 2Keep at least a 2.5 GPA and finish at least 30 semester credit hours (about one full year of classes).
  • Step 3Gather three letters of recommendation from people who know your character.
  • Step 4Get sponsored by a member of the chapter you want to join - a brother has to vouch for you.
  • Step 5Apply online and interview with the Regional Membership Selection Team. They pick men of the highest caliber - and yes, there’s a background check.

Important: applying doesn’t guarantee a spot. Omega chooses carefully, because the letters are for life.

One Rule Above All

Joining Never Hurts

Real talk: joining Omega should never involve pain, embarrassment or being bullied. That’s called hazing, and Omega bans it completely - before, during and after the joining process. Any “brother” who hazes isn’t acting like one. Read our Policies page to know your rights.
