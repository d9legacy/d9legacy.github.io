Omega at Work

Mandated Programs

Every Omega chapter in the world is required to do these. That’s what “mandated” means - no skipping the good stuff.

Achievement Week

Every November, Omega celebrates people who make their communities better - and holds a big essay contest for high school students.

Started way back in 1920 after a speech by historian Carter G. Woodson.

Talent Hunt

A stage for talented young singers, dancers, musicians and actors. Winners get cheers, awards and even college scholarships.

Your school talent show, but bigger.

Scholarship

Omega helps students pay for college and cheers on the brothers with the best grades. Smart is celebrated here.

The fund is named for Dr. Charles Drew, the blood-plasma hero.

Health Initiatives

Brothers host blood drives with the Red Cross and team up with groups fighting cancer and Alzheimer’s to keep communities healthy.

The Charles R. Drew Blood Drive saves lives every year.

Omega STEM

Getting kids everywhere excited about science, technology, engineering and math - the subjects that build the future.

Founder Ernest E. Just would be proud.

Social Action

Chapters roll up their sleeves: building homes, fighting illiteracy, mentoring kids and raising money for good causes.

Uplift is a verb.

Voter Registration

Omega helps people register to vote and learn why every vote counts, all across the country.

A tradition since “Operation Big Vote” in 1976.

Fatherhood & Mentoring

Brothers mentor young people and support dads, because every kid deserves someone in their corner.

Launched nationwide with President Obama’s Fatherhood Initiative.

College Endowment Fund

Every year the fraternity gives at least $50,000 to historically Black colleges and universities - the schools where it all started.

Giving back to places like Howard.

Memorial Service

Every March 12, chapters gather to remember brothers who have passed on to Omega Chapter - the chapter none of us can see.

First held for Colonel Charles Young, who rode 500 miles on horseback to prove he was fit to serve.

Reclamation & Retention

Omega never forgets a brother. This program finds brothers who drifted away and welcomes them back to the fold.

Once a Que, always a Que.

NAACP Partnership

Every chapter keeps a life membership in the NAACP, the nation’s oldest civil rights organization, and brothers are encouraged to join.

Teammates in the fight for fairness since the 1950s.
