The Charles R. Drew Memorial Scholarship Commission

Named After a Hero

Dr. Charles R. Drew was an Omega man and a brilliant doctor whose research made blood banks possible - his work has saved millions of lives. Omega named its international scholarship commission after him. Its mission: celebrate great grades at every level, help chapters and districts give out scholarship money, and encourage research and creativity in everybody, no matter who they are. Every year the commission publishes application forms, guidelines and deadlines for students to apply.
