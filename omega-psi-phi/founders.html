November 17, 1911

Meet the Founders

Three students and one teacher who changed history from a biology office at Howard University.
Ω

Edgar A. Love

Founder • First Grand Basileus

A student from Virginia who became Omega’s very first national president, called the Grand Basileus. After college he became a minister and then a bishop in the Methodist church, leading people his whole life.

Ψ

Oscar J. Cooper

Founder • Grand Keeper of Records

The fraternity’s first secretary and its second Grand Basileus. He became a doctor and cared for patients in Philadelphia for more than 50 years. Imagine helping people feel better for half a century!

Φ

Frank Coleman

Founder • Grand Keeper of Seals

The first treasurer of Omega. He loved science so much that he became the chairman of the Physics Department at Howard University - teaching in the very same halls where Omega was born.

✝

Ernest E. Just

Faculty Adviser • World-Famous Scientist

The biology professor whose office hosted that first meeting. He became one of the most important scientists in the world and was the very first person to win the NAACP’s Spingarn Medal. Proof that teachers can change the world too.

Eleven more students joined as charter members, and Alpha chapter began on December 15, 1911. The founders picked people with big hearts and big brains - and Omega men have been doing the same ever since.
