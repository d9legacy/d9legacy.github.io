Frequently Asked Questions

The questions everybody asks about the Ques, answered plain and simple.

What does Omega Psi Phi mean?

It comes from a Greek phrase: “friendship is essential to the soul.” That’s also the motto.

What’s a “Que”?

A nickname for an Omega man - like a team nickname. You might also hear “Omega Men.”

When was Omega founded?

November 17, 1911, at Howard University - the first fraternity founded at a historically Black college.

Who founded it?

Three students - Edgar A. Love, Oscar J. Cooper and Frank Coleman - with their professor Ernest E. Just.

What are the colors?

Royal purple and old gold. This whole site wears them proudly.

What are the Cardinal Principles?

Manhood, Scholarship, Perseverance and Uplift - the four rules every Omega tries to live by.

What does “lifting as we climb” mean?

When you rise, you reach back and help somebody else rise too. Nobody climbs alone.

Can kids join the real fraternity?

Not yet - you join in college. But kids can enter the essay contest, shine at Talent Hunt, and build the grades and character that make a great Que later.

Are there really famous Ques?

Oh yes: Michael Jordan, Shaquille O’Neal, Langston Hughes, Dr. Charles Drew and more. Meet them on the Famous Ques page.

How do I join the D9Legacy chapter?

Easy: take the quiz. The owner reads every application himself.

Is this the real Omega website?

Nope - this is a loving fan chapter in the D9Legacy universe, made by Josh. The real site is oppf.org.

When is Founders Day?

Every November 17. Cake is strongly encouraged.
