Omega Men of Distinction

Famous Ques

Poets, astronauts, doctors, ballers and world-changers. Omega men show up everywhere.
✎

Langston Hughes

Poet & Writer

One of the greatest American poets ever. His words about dreams - “What happens to a dream deferred?” - are still read in schools everywhere.

✦

Dr. Charles Drew

Doctor & Scientist

Figured out how to store blood plasma, which made blood banks possible. His work has saved millions of lives - maybe even someone you know.

✈

Ronald McNair

Astronaut

A physicist who became an astronaut and flew to space. He died a hero aboard the Space Shuttle Challenger in 1986, and schools across America carry his name.

✊

Bayard Rustin

Civil Rights Leader

The master organizer behind the 1963 March on Washington, where Dr. King gave the “I Have a Dream” speech. He planned one of the biggest peaceful protests in history.

🏀

Michael Jordan

Basketball Legend

Yes, THAT Michael Jordan. Six championships, five MVP awards, and widely called the greatest basketball player of all time. A Que.

🏆

Shaquille O’Neal

Champion & Entertainer

Four-time NBA champion, TV funny man, and Dr. O’Neal - he went back to school and earned a doctorate in education. Scholarship in action.

🎤

Steve Harvey

Comedian & Host

The host of Family Feud and one of the funniest men on television. He tells kids to dream big - and he means it.

⚖

Jesse Jackson

Civil Rights Leader

Marched with Dr. King, ran for president of the United States twice, and spent his life telling young people: “Keep hope alive.”

🏛

L. Douglas Wilder

Governor

In 1990 he became the first elected Black governor in United States history, leading the state of Virginia. A door-opener.

⚑

Colonel Charles Young

Soldier & Trailblazer

One of the first Black colonels in the U.S. Army. When he was told he was unfit to serve, he rode 500 miles on horseback to Washington to prove them wrong. That’s perseverance.

🩺

Dr. David Satcher

Surgeon General

The 16th Surgeon General of the United States - basically the nation’s doctor-in-chief, in charge of keeping the whole country healthy.

🌍

Roy Wilkins

NAACP Leader

Led the NAACP, the nation’s oldest civil rights organization, through some of the most important years in American history.

This is only the beginning - there are Omega men in science labs, courtrooms, classrooms, studios and stadiums all over the world. Maybe your name goes on this page one day.
