About Zeta

Who We Are

Five Pearls founded Zeta in 1920 on one shining idea: finer womanhood.
Our Name

“Scholarship, Service, Sisterhood, Finer Womanhood”

Zeta Phi Beta’s founding ideals are Scholarship, Service, Sisterhood and Finer Womanhood. That last one is the famous Zeta phrase: “finer womanhood” means being your very best self - kind, confident, classy, and strong - and helping every girl around you become her best self too.

The Zeta Ideals

What Every soror Lives By

Scholarship

Books first. Zetas were founded by five honor students, and the standard never dropped.

Service

A “community-conscious, action-oriented” sisterhood - Zetas don’t just care, they DO.

Sisterhood

A bond for life - Zetas are constitutionally bound to their brothers of Phi Beta Sigma, the first such family tie in the Divine Nine.

Finer Womanhood

The famous Zeta ideal: be your finest self - and lift every other woman up with you.

Our Mission

Why Zeta Exists

Zeta Phi Beta calls itself a community-conscious, action-oriented organization. Translation: see a problem, do something about it. Zetas run programs for healthy moms and babies, education, elder care and voting rights - and they do it with their brothers of Phi Beta Sigma, the first such brother-sister bond in Greek life. In the D9Legacy universe, finer is our favorite word.
