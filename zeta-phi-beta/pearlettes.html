For Kids and Teens

The Zeta Youth Auxiliaries

The real Zeta Phi Beta runs official youth auxiliaries so girls can grow up inside the Zeta family - learning scholarship, service and Finer Womanhood at every age. Here is the whole ladder, from littlest to biggest.

The Auxiliaries

Growing Up Zeta

Pearlettes

Little girls

Pearlettes are the youngest Zetas-in-training - little girls learning manners, confidence and kindness, with the sisters as their biggest fans. Tiny pearls, shiny futures.

Amicettes

Girls in the middle years

Amicettes are girls roughly 9 to 13 - old enough to serve, young enough to have a blast doing it. Friendship, fun and first steps in leadership.

Archonettes

High school girls

Archonettes are high school girls leading real service projects and getting ready for college - the finishing stretch before full Zeta life.

Amicae

Women friends of Zeta

Amicae are grown women who serve alongside the sisters as friends of the sorority. Since the 1940s, they have proved you do not need letters to live Finer Womanhood.

Why It Matters

Pearls Grow in Layers

A pearl gets beautiful one thin layer at a time. That is exactly how Zeta grows girls: a Pearlette today, an Amicette next, an Archonette after that - each layer adding shine. The Five Pearls would be proud.
