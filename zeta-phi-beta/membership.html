Our Promise

No Hazing. Ever.

Zeta has a strict zero-hazing rule - and so does D9Legacy. Nobody should ever be hurt, embarrassed, or bullied to join anything. If someone tries that, they are NOT being a real soror, and you should tell a trusted adult right away. Real sorors are chosen for their character, their grades, and their heart - nothing else.
