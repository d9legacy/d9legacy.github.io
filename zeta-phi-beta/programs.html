Z-HOPE

Zetas Helping Other People Excel - health fairs, mentoring, elder care and education programs in neighborhoods everywhere.

Stork’s Nest

With the March of Dimes since the 1970s: helping moms-to-be get checkups, classes and healthy starts for their babies.

Adopt-A-School

Zeta chapters team up with local schools - tutoring, supplies and encouragement for students.

Get Out the Vote

Zetas register voters and help their communities be heard. Your voice counts.

Maternal Health Action

Answering the crisis facing Black mothers and babies - Zetas turned their Founders Day into a global day of service for moms.

Youth Clubs

Amicettes (little girls) and Archonettes (teens) - Zeta’s clubs where young people learn to lead and serve.

These mirror the real sorority’s national programs. Our D9Legacy chapter runs kid-sized versions at Magnolia Crown University.
