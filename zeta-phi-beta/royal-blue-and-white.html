Royal Blue & White

The Story of the Colors

Why royal blue and white mean so much - and what they say about the sisterhood.
The Colors

Royal Blue and White

Zeta Phi Beta's colors are royal blue and white - the blue of deep loyalty and the white of a fresh start. Since 1920, Zetas have worn them with pride, and the whole world knows blue and white means Finer Womanhood.

What They Mean

More Than Pretty

Royal Blue

Loyalty and depth

Zetas and fans often say royal blue stands for loyalty, truth and a sisterhood that runs deep - like the ocean, not a puddle. It is the color of sticking together.

White

Purity and purpose

White stands for a clean heart and a clear purpose - doing the right thing for the right reasons. It is the color of a brand-new page, ready for good work.

Together

Finer Womanhood

Blue and white together carry Zeta's famous ideal: Finer Womanhood - being your best self, then making it finer still. Always growing, always glowing.

The Tradition

The White Dove

Zeta Phi Beta's symbol is the white dove - a bird that stands for peace, hope and love. And the Five Pearls are why Zetas treasure pearls: small beginnings that turn into something beautiful with time and care.

At Magnolia Crown

Blue & White on Campus

In the D9Legacy universe, our Zeta Phi Beta chapter flies royal blue and white all over Magnolia Crown University. When you see those colors on campus, something finer is on the way.
