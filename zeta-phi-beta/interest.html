This page is for the D9Legacy Zeta Beta Chapter at our made-up Magnolia Crown University. It is a kid-friendly fan club, not membership in the real Zeta Phi Beta sorority.

You do not have to know everything yet. Read our history, meet the founders, and explore service. Think about how you can be a kind sister and a good teammate.

  3. Review before you send. Check your answers, add your signature and date, and press Send my letter.
