Everything You Wanted to Know

What do the letters mean?

Zeta Phi Beta (ΖΦΒ) - fans shorten it to Z-Phi-B.

What are Zeta’s colors?

Royal blue and white - crisp and classic.

Why the dove?

The dove is Zeta’s symbol - a sign of peace and gentleness with strength underneath. The flower is the white rose.

Who are the Five Pearls?

The founders: Arizona Cleaver Stemons, Pearl Anna Neal, Myrtle Tyler Faithful, Viola Tyler Goings and Fannie Pettie Watts.

What is finer womanhood?

Zeta’s famous ideal: be your very best self - confident, kind, classy - and lift every other woman up too.

Is Zeta really related to Phi Beta Sigma?

Yes! Zeta was the first sorority constitutionally bound to a fraternity. They’re officially brother and sister organizations.

What is the Stork’s Nest?

A Zeta program with the March of Dimes that helps moms-to-be get care and supplies for healthy babies.

Who was the most famous Zeta author?

Zora Neale Hurston - she wrote “Their Eyes Were Watching God” and helped preserve Black folklore.

How old is Zeta?

Founded in 1920 - the sorority celebrated its 100th birthday in 2020.

Do Zetas only do service?

Service is the action, but sisterhood is the heart: Boules, Founders Day, family events with Phi Beta Sigma, and friends for life.

Is this the real Zeta Phi Beta website?

Nope! This is Josh's D9Legacy fan chapter - a kid-friendly universe built for fun and learning. The real website is zphib1920.org. We just love Zeta here.

Can kids join?

Kids can join the D9Legacy fan chapter right now - take the quiz! The real Zeta Phi Beta is for college students and adults, so school comes first.
