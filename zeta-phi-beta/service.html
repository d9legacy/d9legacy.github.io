Helping Other People Excel

Zeta Phi Beta was built as “a community-conscious, action-oriented organization” - fancy words for: we see a problem, we do something about it. From 1920 to today, Zetas have fed families, taught moms, funded students and served nonstop. Here is the real history, plus our chapter's kid-sized version.

The Real Legacy

A History of Helping

1920

Five Pearls, One Promise

The founders built Zeta during a hard time in American history - and chose service as the answer. That choice still defines the sorority today.

1972

Stork's Nest

Zeta teamed up with the March of Dimes to create Stork's Nest, a program that teaches expecting moms how to have healthy pregnancies and healthy babies. Decades later, it is still going strong.

Every Era

Scholarships for Students

Through its National Educational Foundation, Zeta has given scholarships to generations of students - because a Zeta believes in scholarship for everybody, not just herself.

Today

Z-HOPE

Z-HOPE - Zetas Helping Other People Excel - is the sorority's big service program today, bringing health, education and hope to whole communities. Helping Other People Excel: it says it right in the name.

Our Chapter's Initiatives

Little Z-HOPE Day

The whole chapter

One day each month, the chapter picks someone at school who needs a boost - and boosts them. Notes, help with homework, a saved seat at lunch. Helping other people excel, kid-style.

Story Time Sisters

Chapter readers

Members read to younger kids every week. Because scholarship starts with somebody reading you a story.

Dove Deliveries

Every member

Kindness cards with a little white dove drawn on, delivered to classmates who are sick or sad. Peace, hope and love - envelope edition.

Blue & White Beautify

The whole chapter

The chapter plants flowers and picks up litter around Magnolia Crown. A finer campus for a finer sisterhood.
