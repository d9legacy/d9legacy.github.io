Famous Zetas

Legends in Royal Blue & White

Groundbreaking authors, singers and stars - finer womanhood in the spotlight.
Legends

Names You Should Know

&

Zora Neale Hurston

Groundbreaking Author

Wrote “Their Eyes Were Watching God” - one of the greatest American novels - and studied Black folklore like the scholar she was.

&

Esther Rolle

Beloved Actress

Florida Evans on “Good Times” - America’s TV mom and an Emmy winner.

&

Sarah Vaughan

Jazz Legend

“Sassy” - one of the greatest jazz voices ever, with a range that could stop time.

&

Dionne Warwick

Music Icon (Honorary)

Fifty years of hits - “Walk On By,” “That’s What Friends Are For” - and a heart for charity.

&

Sheryl Underwood

Comedian & TV Host

A star of “The Talk” who served Zeta as International President - funny AND finer.

&

Violette Neatley Anderson

Legal Pioneer

The first Black woman to argue before the U.S. Supreme Court (1926) - and a Zeta president.

&

Gwendolyn Brooks

Pulitzer Prize Poet (Honorary)

The first African American to win the Pulitzer Prize for poetry (1950).

&

Towanda Braxton

Singer & TV Star

Member of the Braxton family - music and reality TV royalty.

&

Chrystee Pharris

Actress

Star of “Passions” and “Scrubs” - representing the blue and white on screen.

Plus generations of Zeta teachers, nurses, judges and organizers living finer womanhood every day.
