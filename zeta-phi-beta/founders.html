The Founders of Zeta

The Five Pearls

Five Howard University students who founded Zeta on January 16, 1920.
The Pearls

Meet the Five

&

Arizona Cleaver Stemons

Founder (Pearl)

A teacher and social worker who dreamed up the sorority - the first of the five Pearls.

&

Pearl Anna Neal

Founder (Pearl)

A music student and teacher - she shared the idea and helped make it real.

&

Myrtle Tyler Faithful

Founder (Pearl)

A teacher remembered for her elegance - finer womanhood in person.

&

Viola Tyler Goings

Founder (Pearl)

Myrtle’s sister - she helped shape the sorority’s first traditions.

&

Fannie Pettie Watts

Founder (Pearl)

A teacher and the sorority’s first secretary - keeper of the earliest records.

The five Pearls were students at Howard University. They asked Phi Beta Sigma’s founders for advice - and the two organizations have been family ever since.
