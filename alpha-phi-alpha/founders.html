The Founders of Alpha

The Seven Jewels

Seven Cornell students who founded the first on December 4, 1906.
The Jewels

Meet the Seven

&

Henry Arthur Callis

Jewel

A brilliant student who became a doctor - and the last surviving Jewel, living into the 1970s.

&

Charles Henry Chapman

Jewel

A teacher and college professor who spent his life in classrooms lifting students up.

&

Eugene Kinckle Jones

Jewel

A giant of the National Urban League - he spent 30 years fighting for jobs and fair housing.

&

George Biddle Kelley

Jewel

The fraternity’s first president - a civil engineer who helped design the young brotherhood.

&

Nathaniel Allison Murray

Jewel

A teacher and farmer who stayed connected to the fraternity his whole life.

&

Robert Harold Ogle

Jewel

A lawyer and Senate clerk in Washington - sharp, steady, and first-class.

&

Vertner Woodson Tandy

Jewel

The first Black registered architect in New York State - he designed the fraternity’s famous pin.

All seven were students at Cornell University. Their study group in 1905 became the fraternity in 1906 - the first Black intercollegiate fraternity in America.
