Everything You Wanted to Know

What do the letters A-Phi-A mean?

Alpha Phi Alpha (ΑΦΑ) - the first letter of the Greek alphabet twice, because they were the FIRST. It fits.

Was Alpha really the first?

Yes! Founded December 4, 1906, Alpha Phi Alpha is the first African American intercollegiate fraternity. Every other D9 org came after.

Who are the Seven Jewels?

The seven founders: Callis, Chapman, Jones, Kelley, Murray, Ogle and Tandy. They’re called Jewels because the fraternity treasures them.

What are Alpha’s colors?

Black and old gold - classic and sharp.

Why the sphinx?

The Great Sphinx of Giza is ancient, wise and has lasted thousands of years. Alphas aim to build things that last too.

What does the motto mean?

“First of All, Servants of All, We Shall Transcend All” - first in line, helping everybody, rising above everything.

Was MLK really an Alpha?

Yes! Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. was a proud brother of Alpha Phi Alpha - so was Justice Thurgood Marshall and Jesse Owens.

What is A-Phi-A’s most famous program?

“Go to High School, Go to College” - since 1922, brothers have pushed students to keep climbing.

How old is Alpha?

Founded in 1906 - the fraternity turns 120 years old in December 2026. Oldest of the Divine Nine.

Do Alphas only do service?

Service is the job, but brotherhood is the joy: conventions, step shows, Founders Day and friends for life.

Is this the real Alpha Phi Alpha website?

Nope! This is Josh's D9Legacy fan chapter - a kid-friendly universe built for fun and learning. The real website is apa1906.net. We just love Alpha here.

Can kids join?

Kids can join the D9Legacy fan chapter right now - take the quiz! The real Alpha Phi Alpha is for college students and adults, so school comes first.
