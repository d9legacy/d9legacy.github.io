Famous Alphas

Legends in Black & Old Gold

A Nobel Peace Prize winner, a Justice, an Olympian and a Duke - Alpha men changed the world.
Legends

Names You Should Know

&

Martin Luther King Jr.

Civil Rights Icon

Led the civil rights movement, won the Nobel Peace Prize, and gave the “I Have a Dream” speech. Brother King changed America forever.

&

Thurgood Marshall

Supreme Court Justice

Won Brown v. Board of Education, ending school segregation - then became the first Black Justice on the U.S. Supreme Court.

&

W.E.B. Du Bois

Scholar & NAACP Founder

A brilliant writer and thinker who helped found the NAACP and wrote “The Souls of Black Folk.”

&

Jesse Owens

Olympic Legend

Won four gold medals at the 1936 Olympics in Berlin, right in front of Hitler. The fastest man in the world.

&

Duke Ellington

Jazz Royalty

One of the greatest musicians in American history - the Duke made jazz into art.

&

Paul Robeson

Singer, Actor, Athlete

A true everything-man: All-American football player, lawyer, singer and actor with a voice like thunder.

&

Andrew Young

Ambassador & Mayor

Civil rights leader, U.S. Ambassador to the United Nations, and mayor of Atlanta.

&

Lionel Richie

Music Superstar

The voice behind “Hello” and “All Night Long” - yes, he’s an Alpha.

&

Kwame Nkrumah

President of Ghana

Led Ghana to independence in 1957 and became its first president - Alpha goes global.

Plus countless Alpha judges, generals, scientists, mayors and coaches. When your motto says “first,” you collect a lot of firsts.
