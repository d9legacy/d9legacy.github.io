About Alpha

Who We Are

The first - seven students at Cornell started the very first Black intercollegiate fraternity in 1906.
Our Name

“First of All, Servants of All, We Shall Transcend All”

Alpha Phi Alpha’s motto is a mouthful: “First of All, Servants of All, We Shall Transcend All.” Break it down: they were the FIRST (the first Black intercollegiate fraternity), they SERVE (helping others is the job), and they TRANSCEND (rise above every obstacle). Say it three times fast - then go live it.

Quick Facts

Alpha at a Glance

  • FoundedDecember 4, 1906 at Cornell University in Ithaca, New York.
  • The FirstThe first African American intercollegiate fraternity - the original Divine Nine organization.
  • The JewelsThe seven founders are called the Seven Jewels. That’s how much they’re treasured.
  • ColorsBlack and old gold - sharp, classic, and serious about excellence.
  • SymbolThe Great Sphinx of Giza - ancient, wise, and built to last forever.
  • NicknamesMembers are called Alphas or Alpha Men. The fraternity’s nickname: A-Phi-A.
  • FamilyThe founding member of the Divine Nine. See all nine at D9Legacy.
Our Mission

Why Alpha Exists

Alpha Phi Alpha develops leaders. That’s the whole mission: take smart young men, train them to serve, and send them out to change the world - and it works. Alphas led the civil rights movement, fought for voters, and built programs that send kids to college. In the D9Legacy universe, we follow the same rule: first to help, first to learn, first to lead.
