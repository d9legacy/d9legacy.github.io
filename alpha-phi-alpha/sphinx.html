The Sphinx

The Keeper of Wisdom

The ancient symbol, the famous magazine, and what they mean to Alpha.
The Symbol

Why the Sphinx?

Alpha Phi Alpha's symbol is the Great Sphinx - the enormous stone statue of Egypt, with the body of a lion and the head of a king. It has stood in the African desert for thousands of years, through storms and empires, and it is still standing. Fans say that is exactly the point: wisdom and strength that outlast everything.

The founders chose their symbols on purpose. A sphinx from Africa, the continent where human civilization began, for the first Black intercollegiate fraternity in America. That is not a small thought - that is a huge one.

The Magazine

A Sphinx You Can Read

The Sphinx is not just a statue - it is also the name of the real fraternity's official magazine, first published in 1914. That makes it one of the oldest Black magazines in America, carrying Alpha news, history and big ideas for more than 110 years. A keeper of wisdom, in print.

Fun Facts

Sphinx Did-You-Knows

A Century in Print

Older than World War I's end

The Sphinx magazine started in 1914 - before radios were in living rooms, before airplanes had crossed the ocean. It is still publishing today.

An African Wonder

One of the world's oldest statues

The real Great Sphinx in Egypt is one of the oldest and biggest statues on Earth - carved from a single ridge of stone thousands of years ago.

Seven Jewels, One Sphinx

Symbols that fit together

The founders are called the Jewels - treasures. The symbol is the Sphinx - a guardian of treasure. Seven treasures, guarded by wisdom.
