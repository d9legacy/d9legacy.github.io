All Nine Organization Spotlights

One card for every Divine Nine organization - the real facts plus how each one lives on D9Legacy.

D9Legacy is fan-made. The real history on this page is true; the D9Legacy chapters, owners and stories are our own pretend universe.

Alpha Phi Alpha ΑΦΑ

Founded: December 4, 1906 at Cornell University

Colors: Black and Old Gold

Motto: “First of All, Servants of All, We Shall Transcend All”

Known for: the Great Sphinx

The very first Divine Nine organization, started by seven college men known as the Jewels. Our own Josh is the chapter owner.

Visit ΑΦΑ

Alpha Kappa Alpha ΑΚΑ

Founded: January 15, 1908 at Howard University

Colors: Salmon Pink and Apple Green

Motto: “By Culture and By Merit”

Known for: the Ivy Leaf

The first Greek-letter sorority founded by African American women. Jaylee D. Strong founded our chapter, and Ahriyah Moore was the first member accepted.

Visit ΑΚΑ

Kappa Alpha Psi ΚΑΨ

Founded: January 5, 1911 at Indiana University

Colors: Crimson and Cream

Motto: “Achievement in Every Field of Human Endeavor”

Known for: the Diamond and the Kappa Kane

Founded as Kappa Alpha Nu and renamed in 1915. Home of the famous Kappa Kane. CJ Smith owns our chapter.

Visit ΚΑΨ

Omega Psi Phi ΩΨΦ

Founded: November 17, 1911 at Howard University

Colors: Royal Purple and Old Gold

Motto: “Friendship Is Essential to the Soul”

Known for: the Lamp

Built on four cardinal principles: Manhood, Scholarship, Perseverance and Uplift. This was Josh's first chapter before he moved to Alpha.

Visit ΩΨΦ

Delta Sigma Theta ΔΣΘ

Founded: January 13, 1913 at Howard University

Colors: Crimson and Cream

Motto: “Intelligence is the Torch of Wisdom”

Known for: the Pyramid

Twenty-two women founded Delta and marched for women's right to vote just weeks later. Kinte owns our chapter.

Visit ΔΣΘ

Phi Beta Sigma ΦΒΣ

Founded: January 9, 1914 at Howard University

Colors: Royal Blue and Pure White

Motto: “Culture For Service and Service For Humanity”

Known for: the Dove

Founded to be a fraternity of the people, built on brotherhood, scholarship and service.

Visit ΦΒΣ

Zeta Phi Beta ΖΦΒ

Founded: January 16, 1920 at Howard University

Colors: Royal Blue and White

Motto: “Scholarship, Service, Sisterhood and Finer Womanhood”

Known for: the White Dove

Founded by five women known as the Pearls. Zeta and Phi Beta Sigma are constitutionally bound as brother and sister organizations.

Visit ΖΦΒ

Sigma Gamma Rho ΣΓΡ

Founded: November 12, 1922 at Butler University

Colors: Royal Blue and Gold

Motto: “Greater Service, Greater Progress”

Known for: the Golden Poodle

Founded by seven schoolteachers, and the only D9 sorority founded at a mostly-white university. Ivory Cooper owns our chapter.

Visit ΣΓΡ

Iota Phi Theta ΙΦΘ

Founded: September 19, 1963 at Morgan State University

Colors: Charcoal Brown and Gilded Gold

Motto: “Building A Tradition, Not Resting Upon One!”

Known for: the Centaur

The youngest of the Divine Nine, founded during the Civil Rights Movement by twelve men at Morgan State.

Visit ΙΦΘ
