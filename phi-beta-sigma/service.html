Culture for Service and Service for Humanity

The Motto Is the Mission

Some mottos are just words. Sigma's is a to-do list: “Culture for Service and Service for Humanity.” From 1914 to today, Sigmas have fed the hungry, housed the homeless, registered voters and mentored boys. Here is the real history, plus our chapter's kid-sized version.

The Real Legacy

A History of Helping

1914

Built to Include

The founders wanted a fraternity that served the whole community instead of just itself - “the people’s fraternity.” Service was not a program. It was the point.

1930s

Bigger and Better Business

Sigma launched its Bigger and Better Business program to support Black-owned businesses during some of America's hardest years - teaching money smarts and buying together.

1980s

Sleep Out for the Homeless

Sigmas began sleeping outside on cold nights for Sleep Out for the Homeless, raising money and attention for people with nowhere warm to go. One uncomfortable night, so others could be seen.

Today

Sigma Wellness and Mentoring

Today's brothers run health programs, partner with the March of Dimes, and mentor thousands of boys through the Sigma Beta Club. Service for humanity, still on schedule.

At Magnolia Crown

Our Chapter's Initiatives

Snack Pack Saturdays

The whole chapter

Once a month the brothers pack snack bags for kids who get hungry on weekends. Small bag, big difference.

Piggy Bank Class

Inspired by Bigger and Better Business

The chapter teaches younger kids simple money smarts - save some, share some, spend some. Business class, but fun.

Coat and Blanket Drive

Inspired by Sleep Out for the Homeless

Every winter the brothers collect coats and blankets for families who need them. Nobody at Magnolia Crown should be cold.

Little Brother Lunch

Chapter mentors

Members eat lunch once a week with younger boys who want a big brother to look up to. Mentoring, one sandwich at a time.

Your Turn

Service Starts Small

Share your snack. Teach a friend something. That is service for humanity already. Our initiatives are part of the fan-made D9Legacy universe - the real fraternity's programs are at pbs1914.org.
