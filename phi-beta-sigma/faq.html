Everything You Wanted to Know

What do the letters mean?

Phi Beta Sigma (ΦΒΣ) - the fraternity founded so nobody would be left out.

What are Sigma’s colors?

Royal blue and pure white.

What does GOMAB mean?

It’s the famous Sigma salute: “Go Out and Make the Brotherhood” - a cheer brothers shout with pride.

Is Sigma really related to Zeta Phi Beta?

Yes! Sigma brothers helped found Zeta in 1920, and the two are constitutionally bound - the first official brother-sister bond in Greek life.

What is the Sigma Beta Club?

Sigma’s mentoring club for young boys - grades, character and service, taught by the brothers.

Who wrote the Black national anthem?

Brother James Weldon Johnson wrote “Lift Every Voice and Sing.” Yes - THAT song.

What are the three pillars?

Bigger and Better Business, Education, and Social Action - Sigma’s three big national programs.

Was John Lewis a Sigma?

Yes - Congressman John Lewis, hero of Bloody Sunday, was a brother of Phi Beta Sigma.

How old is Sigma?

Founded in 1914 - the fraternity celebrated its 100th birthday in 2014.

Do Sigmas only do service?

Service is the mission, but brotherhood is the fun: Founders Day, Sigma Week, family events with the Zetas, and brothers for life.

Is this the real Phi Beta Sigma website?

Nope! This is Josh's D9Legacy fan chapter - a kid-friendly universe built for fun and learning. The real website is pbs1914.org. We just love Sigma here.

Can kids join?

Kids can join the D9Legacy fan chapter right now - take the quiz! The real Phi Beta Sigma is for college students and adults, so school comes first.
