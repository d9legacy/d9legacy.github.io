The History of Sigma

How It All Began

On January 9, 1914, three young men at Howard University - A. Langston Taylor, Leonard F. Morse and Charles I. Brown - founded Phi Beta Sigma. They had seen clubs that kept people out, and they wanted the opposite: a brotherhood where every member truly belonged, built to serve all humanity. A few years later they helped five young women found Zeta Phi Beta, and the two families have been bound together ever since.
