For Boys and Young Men

The Sigma Beta Club

The real Phi Beta Sigma runs a national youth mentoring program called the Sigma Beta Club - clubs where boys ages 8 to 18 learn leadership, school smarts, healthy habits and how to be a good man, with Sigma brothers as their mentors.

What Members Do

Four Big Lessons

School First

Scholarship

Sigma Beta Club members work on grades, reading and college dreams. Homework help is always on the menu.

Lead Something

Leadership

Members run their own little projects with the brothers coaching from the sideline. You learn to lead by actually leading.

Serve Somebody

Service

Every club does community service - because a Sigma starts serving long before he is old enough to wear the letters.

Grow Up Right

Character

Respect, honesty, health and good choices. The club is really about becoming somebody your family is proud of.

Why It Matters

Boys Need Brothers

The founders believed a fraternity should pour into its community, and there is no better investment than a kid. Thousands of men today got their first mentor, first applause and first big dream inside a Sigma Beta Club.
