Famous Sigmas

Legends in Royal Blue & White

Anthem writers, freedom fighters and NFL stars - Sigma men go hard for humanity.
Legends

Names You Should Know

&

James Weldon Johnson

Writer of the Anthem

Wrote “Lift Every Voice and Sing” - the Black national anthem - and led the NAACP. Every Sigma knows his words by heart.

&

John Lewis

Congressman & Hero

Led the march across the Edmund Pettus Bridge on Bloody Sunday, then served in Congress for over 30 years. “Good trouble” in person.

&

George Washington Carver

Scientist (Honorary)

The plant genius of Tuskegee - hundreds of uses for the peanut and a lifetime of teaching farmers.

&

Huey P. Newton

Activist

Co-founded the Black Panther Party - its free breakfast program fed thousands of kids.

&

Al Roker

America’s Weatherman

The “Today Show” legend who has wished America good morning for decades.

&

Emmitt Smith

NFL Legend

The NFL’s all-time leading rusher - three Super Bowls with the Dallas Cowboys.

&

Jerry Rice

Greatest Receiver Ever

Widely called the greatest wide receiver in football history - 1,549 catches and three rings.

&

Blair Underwood

Actor

Star of “L.A. Law” and a hundred screens since - blue and white on the red carpet.

&

Terrence Howard

Actor

Oscar-nominated star of “Hustle & Flow” and “Empire.”

Plus generations of Sigma educators, soldiers, judges and coaches serving humanity every day.
