About Sigma

Who We Are

Three founders built Sigma in 1914 so that every brother would belong - and every community would be served.
Our Name

“Culture For Service and Service For Humanity”

Phi Beta Sigma’s motto is “Culture For Service and Service For Humanity.” The founders believed knowledge isn’t for showing off - it’s for spending on other people. Get smart, get cultured, then pour it all into humanity. That’s the Sigma way.

The Sigma Way

What Every brother Lives By

Brotherhood

Three founders built a family where every member belongs - brothers for life.

Scholarship

Sharp minds on purpose. Sigmas treat learning like training for the big game.

Service

Service for humanity - not for show, not for credit. For people.

Inclusion

Founded so NO member would ever feel left out - Sigma was built to include, not to exclude.

Quick Facts

Sigma at a Glance

  • FoundedJanuary 9, 1914 at Howard University in Washington, D.C.
  • The ThreeFounded by three men: A. Langston Taylor, Leonard F. Morse and Charles I. Brown.
  • ColorsRoyal blue and pure white.
  • SymbolThe dove - just like their sister organization.
  • Family TieConstitutionally bound to Zeta Phi Beta Sorority - the first brother-sister bond in Greek life.
  • NicknamesMembers are called Sigmas or Frat (that means brother).
  • FamilyOne of the Divine Nine. See all nine at D9Legacy.
Our Mission

Why Sigma Exists

Phi Beta Sigma lives by brotherhood, scholarship and service. The founders wanted a fraternity that included everybody and served everybody - “culture for service and service for humanity.” Today Sigmas run businesses programs, education drives and social action campaigns, side by side with their sisters of Zeta Phi Beta. In the D9Legacy universe, blue and white means: everybody in, nobody left out.
