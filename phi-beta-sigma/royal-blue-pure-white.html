Royal Blue & Pure White

The Story of the Colors

Why royal blue and pure white mean so much - and what they say about the brotherhood.
The Colors

Royal Blue and Pure White

Phi Beta Sigma's colors are royal blue and pure white, chosen by the founders in 1914. Walk onto almost any campus in America and that blue and white means one thing: the Sigmas are here, and they came to serve.

What They Mean

More Than Pretty

Royal Blue

Loyalty and brotherhood

Sigmas and fans often say royal blue stands for loyalty - brothers who stay true to each other and to their communities, no matter what. It is the color of showing up.

Pure White

Pure hearts, pure motives

Pure white stands for serving with a clean heart - helping people because it is right, not because it gets you applause. Service with no strings attached.

Together

Culture for service

Blue and white together carry the motto: Culture for Service and Service for Humanity. Everything the brotherhood does - every program, every project - starts from those two colors' promise.

The Tradition

The Dove Flies Blue

Because Zeta Phi Beta is Sigma's sister organization, the two share more than history - you will spot the dove, the royal blue and the white in both families. One house, two rooms: brothers in blue, sisters in blue and white.

At Magnolia Crown

Blue & White on Campus

In the D9Legacy universe, our Phi Beta Sigma chapter flies royal blue and pure white all over Magnolia Crown University. When the blue and white shows up, service is about to happen.
