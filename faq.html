D9Legacy FAQ

Everything kids (and grown-ups) ask about the D9Legacy universe.

What is D9Legacy?

D9Legacy is a fan-made, kid-friendly website about the Divine Nine - the nine Black Greek-letter organizations. Josh founded it in 2026 so kids can learn the history, pick a chapter and start a legacy.

Is D9Legacy an official website?

No. D9Legacy is fan-made and is not connected to the real Alpha Kappa Alpha, Alpha Phi Alpha or any other real organization. For the real organizations, visit their official websites.

Is the history on this site real?

The founding dates, founders, colors and mottos are real history. The D9Legacy chapters, owners and characters (like Jaylee D. Strong and Ahriyah Moore) are our own pretend stories - and our pages say so.

How do I join a chapter?

Go to the D9Legacy home page and take the join quiz, or visit a chapter site and tap Join. You will fill out the quiz AND the chapter's interest sheet. Pick Omega, stay on Omega - each chapter keeps you on its own site.

Do the forms really send my answers to someone?

No. Right now, everything you fill out is saved only on the device you used. The chapter owners cannot see it on their own devices. Never put private information in any form.

Who can I contact with questions?

Text the D9Legacy line at (678) 734-0325 or use the Contact Leadership page. You can also follow and message @d9legacy7 on TikTok.

Who runs the chapters?

Josh (Joshua Samuel) founded D9Legacy and owns the Alpha Phi Alpha chapter. Jaylee D. Strong owns AKA, Kinte owns Delta Sigma Theta, CJ Smith owns Kappa Alpha Psi and Ivory Cooper owns Sigma Gamma Rho. The rest are open!

What does 'With Jesus Christ' mean on the pages?

Josh asked for it to be on the site's sheets and letters. It is a reminder that everything here is built with faith.

What is the Owner Dashboard?

A pretend control room where chapter owners can practice editing site info, approving interest letters and posting announcements. It is a demo - everything stays on your own device.

Can I get a certificate?

Yes! Visit the Certificates page, pick your chapter, type your name and download a real picture file of your certificate to keep or print.
