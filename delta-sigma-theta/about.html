About Delta

Who We Are

Twenty-two young women started Delta in 1913 - and their very first act was marching for what’s right.
Our Name

“Intelligence is the Torch of Wisdom”

Delta Sigma Theta’s motto is “Intelligence is the Torch of Wisdom.” Think of your brain as a flashlight: the more you learn, the brighter it shines. And a torch isn’t just for you - it lights the path so other people can find their way too. That is what Deltas do: learn a lot, then use it to help everybody.

The Five-Point Programmatic Thrust

What Every soror Lives By

Economic Development

Helping families and neighborhoods learn about money, save smart, and build businesses that lift everybody up.

Educational Development

Tutoring, mentoring and scholarships - because every kid deserves a fair shot at learning.

International Awareness

Caring about people all over the world, not just next door. Deltas help communities across the globe.

Physical & Mental Health

Healthy bodies and healthy minds matter. Deltas teach families how to take care of both.

Political Awareness

Making sure every voice is heard: registering voters and speaking up for fairness.

Quick Facts

Delta at a Glance

  • FoundedJanuary 13, 1913 at Howard University in Washington, D.C., by 22 brave young women.
  • First ActTheir very first public act was marching for women’s right to vote in the 1913 Women’s Suffrage Parade - just weeks after founding.
  • ColorsCrimson and cream. Bold red and soft cream, just like this website.
  • SymbolThe elephant - strong, wise, and never forgets. The flower is the African violet.
  • NicknamesMembers are called Deltas or sorors (that means sisters).
  • FamilyOne of the Divine Nine, the nine historic Black Greek-letter organizations. See them all at D9Legacy.
  • SizeOne of the largest sororities in the world, with hundreds of thousands of members and over 1,000 chapters around the globe.
Our Mission

Why Delta Exists

Delta Sigma Theta is a public service sorority. That means the whole point is helping people. Every chapter works on the Five-Point Programmatic Thrust: money smarts, education, world awareness, health, and political voice. From feeding families to fighting for voting rights, Deltas show up. In the D9Legacy universe, our chapter does the same - one good deed at a time.
