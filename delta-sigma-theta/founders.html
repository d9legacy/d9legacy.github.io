The Founders of Delta

The 22 Founders

Twenty-two Howard University students who changed history on January 13, 1913.
Sisterhood of 1913

Meet the Founders

&

Osceola Macarthy Adams

Founder

An actress who helped start American theater companies - one of Delta’s boldest creative spirits.

&

Marguerite Young Alexander

Founder

A French and Spanish teacher who spent her life helping students love language and learning.

&

Winona Cargile Alexander

Founder

The very first president of Delta’s Alpha chapter - the sister who led the way.

&

Ethel Cuff Black

Founder

A teacher and leader who helped Delta grow across the country in its earliest years.

&

Bertha Pitts Campbell

Founder

A suffrage marcher who later led a huge voter registration drive - and lived to see Delta turn 75.

&

Zephyr Chisom Carter

Founder

A singer and performer who brought music and joy to the young sisterhood.

&

Edna Brown Coleman

Founder

A dedicated teacher who educated children for decades after graduating from Howard.

&

Jessie McGuire Dent

Founder

A Texas teacher who fought in court for equal pay for Black teachers - and won.

&

Frederica Chase Dodd

Founder

A social worker who spent her career helping families in need across the country.

&

Myra Davis Hemmings

Founder

Delta’s first national president - she guided the baby sorority’s first big steps.

&

Olive C. Jones

Founder

A gifted pianist and teacher who shared music with her students for years.

&

Jimmie Bugg Middleton

Founder

A champion of libraries - she helped lead the fight for fair library access for everyone.

&

Pauline Oberdorfer Minor

Founder

Delta’s first national treasurer - the money-smart sister who kept the books.

&

Vashti Turley Murphy

Founder

A teacher and mother whose own daughters later helped lead the sorority too.

&

Naomi Sewell Richardson

Founder

The last surviving founder - she celebrated Delta’s 80th birthday in 1993.

&

Mamie Reddy Rose

Founder

A warm-hearted teacher remembered for her kindness to her Howard classmates.

&

Eliza Pearl Shippen

Founder

A brilliant student who went on to teach English and inspire young readers.

&

Florence Letcher Toms

Founder

A collector of Delta history who helped save the founders’ stories for us.

&

Ethel Carr Watson

Founder

A teacher who guided Delta’s earliest rituals and traditions with care.

&

Wertie Blackwell Weaver

Founder

A writer for Delta’s early publications - one of the sisterhood’s first storytellers.

&

Madree Penn White

Founder

A journalist and editor who became Delta’s second national president.

&

Edith Motte Young

Founder

A teacher from North Carolina who helped shape Delta’s very first chapter.

All 22 founders were students at Howard University. Their first public act together was marching in the 1913 Women’s Suffrage Parade - service from day one.
