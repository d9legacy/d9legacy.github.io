Scholarships & Programs

Chapter Scholarships

Local Delta chapters across the country award college scholarships to graduating high school seniors every single year. Good grades and community service count.

Delta Academy & GEMS

Not money - something just as good: mentors who help girls in middle and high school build the skills (and the college applications) that win scholarships later.

National Awards

The national sorority and its regions honor top students with scholarships and recognition at big conventions and regional conferences.

Emergency Help

Many chapters keep funds to help students with books, fees and surprise costs so a small bill never ends a big dream.

Real scholarship details come from your local chapter - ask a teacher or counselor to help you find the nearest Delta chapter’s program.

Kids Can Get Ready

You can start winning scholarships years before college: keep your grades up, read every day, help your neighbors, and save your best work. That is exactly what scholarship judges look for. The D9Legacy chapter’s own quiz is practice - show us your stuff!
