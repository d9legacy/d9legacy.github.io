Mandated Programs

Delta at Work

Every Delta chapter runs real programs that help real people. Here is what the work looks like.
Five-Point Programmatic Thrust

Delta at Work

Economic Development

Money smarts for everybody: classes on saving, budgeting, buying homes, and supporting Black-owned businesses.

Educational Development

Tutoring, mentoring, and scholarships so every student can climb higher. School is the Delta love language.

International Awareness

Helping communities around the world - from schools in Africa to clean water projects across the globe.

Physical & Mental Health

Health fairs, exercise challenges, and honest talks about feelings - because strong minds need strong bodies.

Political Awareness

Delta Days in the Nation’s Capital, voter registration drives, and speaking up for fairness in every community.

Delta Academy

A club for girls age 11-14: science, math, leadership and confidence, taught by Delta mentors.

Delta GEMS

Growing and Empowering Myself Successfully - a program that helps teen girls dream big and plan for college.

EMBODI

Empowering Males to Build Opportunities for Developing Independence - mentoring for teen boys, led by Delta women.

These mirror the real sorority’s Five-Point Programmatic Thrust and youth programs. Our D9Legacy chapter runs kid-sized versions at Magnolia Crown University.
