Famous Deltas

Legends in Crimson & Cream

Congresswomen, queens of soul, poets and pioneers - Delta women have done it all.
Legends

Names You Should Know

&

Shirley Chisholm

Trailblazing Congresswoman

The first Black woman ever elected to the U.S. Congress (1968) and the first Black woman to run for a major party’s presidential nomination. “Unbought and unbossed.”

&

Dorothy Height

Civil Rights Leader

The “godmother of the civil rights movement” - she led the National Council of Negro Women for 40 years and advised presidents.

&

Mary McLeod Bethune

Educator (Honorary)

Founded a school that became Bethune-Cookman University and advised President Franklin D. Roosevelt. One of America’s greatest educators.

&

Barbara Jordan

Congresswoman

The first Black woman from the South elected to Congress, famous for her powerful voice during the Watergate hearings.

&

Aretha Franklin

Queen of Soul (Honorary)

The voice behind “Respect” - 18 Grammy Awards and the first woman in the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame.

&

Cicely Tyson

Legendary Actress

An award-winning actress who spent 70 years playing strong, honest characters on stage and screen.

&

Ruby Dee

Actress & Activist

Star of stage and screen who marched for civil rights alongside her husband, Ossie Davis.

&

Nikki Giovanni

Poet

One of America’s most beloved poets - her words about Black joy and strength fill whole libraries.

&

Leontyne Price

Opera Legend (Honorary)

A Mississippi-born singer with one of the greatest voices in opera history - and a neighbor to our founder Josh’s home state.

That is only the beginning - thousands of Delta women lead in science, sports, medicine, business and the arts.
