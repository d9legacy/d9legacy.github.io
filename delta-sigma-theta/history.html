The History of Delta

A Sisterhood Born to Serve

From a Howard University dorm room to the biggest stages in the world.
The Beginning

Twenty-Two Women, One Big Idea

On January 13, 1913, twenty-two young women at Howard University in Washington, D.C. founded Delta Sigma Theta. They wanted a sisterhood that didn’t just make friends - it made a difference. And they proved it fast: that March, the brand-new Deltas marched in the great Women’s Suffrage Parade in Washington, standing up for women’s right to vote when many people told them to stay home. Serving bravely from day one - that is the Delta way.

The Timeline

Delta Through the Years

1913

The Beginning

Twenty-two founders start Delta Sigma Theta at Howard University on January 13. Weeks later they march in the Women’s Suffrage Parade - the sorority’s first public act.

1919 - 1930s

Growing Nationwide

Chapters spread to colleges and cities across America. In 1930 the sorority is officially incorporated as a national public service organization.

1930s - 1950s

Library Books & Civil Rights

Deltas open libraries for Black communities in the South, fight for fair schools, and leaders like Dorothy Height begin decades of civil rights work.

1960s

The Movement Years

Delta women are on the front lines: marching in 1963’s March on Washington, fighting for the vote, and opening doors in Congress - Shirley Chisholm becomes the first Black woman elected to Congress in 1968.

1970s - 1990s

Bigger Than Ever

Delta grows into one of the largest Black women’s organizations in the world, launching youth mentoring like Delta Academy and health programs for families.

2000s - Today

A Global Sisterhood

More than 350,000 members in over 1,000 chapters across America and the world keep the five-point mission going - and our D9Legacy chapter cheers them on.

Why It Matters

Still Marching

Over 113 years later, Delta women are still doing what the founders did: learning hard, serving bravely, and speaking up. The torch of wisdom keeps getting passed - and in the D9Legacy universe, it’s burning bright.
