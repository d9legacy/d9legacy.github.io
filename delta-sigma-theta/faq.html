Everything You Wanted to Know

What does Delta Sigma Theta mean?

It comes from Greek letters: Delta (Δ), Sigma (Σ), Theta (Θ). The founders picked Greek letters the way other college clubs do - and made them stand for service.

What are Delta’s colors?

Crimson and cream - a deep bold red and a soft warm cream. You’re looking at them right now.

Why is the elephant Delta’s symbol?

Elephants are strong, smart, loyal and never forget their family - exactly like Delta sisters. (Ask nicely and a Delta might explain the trunk-up thing.)

What is a soror?

It means sister! Members call each other “soror,” like “sister” with extra sparkle.

What is the Five-Point Programmatic Thrust?

The five kinds of service every Delta chapter works on: economic development, education, international awareness, physical and mental health, and political awareness.

Was Delta really in the suffrage parade?

Yes! In March 1913, the brand-new founders marched in the great Women’s Suffrage Parade in Washington, D.C. It was their first public act - before the sorority was even three months old.

Who was the first Delta president?

Founder Myra Davis Hemmings was the first national president, back when the whole sorority was just getting started.

How big is Delta?

One of the biggest sororities on Earth: hundreds of thousands of members and over 1,000 chapters in America and around the world.

Who owns the D9Legacy Delta chapter?

Kinte - appointed by Josh, who founded the whole D9Legacy universe in 2026. He runs a tight, kind ship.

Do Deltas only do service?

Service is the mission, but sisterhood is the glue: step shows, conventions, Founders Day parties and lifelong best friends.

Is this the real Delta Sigma Theta website?

Nope! This is Josh's D9Legacy fan chapter - a kid-friendly universe built for fun and learning. The real website is deltasigmatheta.org. We just love Delta here.

Can kids join?

Kids can join the D9Legacy fan chapter right now - take the quiz! The real Delta Sigma Theta is for college students and adults, so school comes first.
