The Real Path

The Real Way In

Go to College

The real sorority recruits at colleges and universities. So step one for any future Delta: do great in school and plan for college.

Keep Your Grades Up

Chapters look for students with solid grades - usually a certain GPA or better. Brains before everything.

Show Your Heart

Delta is a public service sorority, so volunteer work matters. Help your community and keep the receipts - chapters notice.

Get to Know the Chapter

Students attend public events, meet the sorors, and let the chapter get to know them. Friendship first.

The Selection Process

When a chapter opens membership intake, students apply and are chosen for character, grades and service. It is never a hazing process - ever.

Alumnae Chapters

College graduates can join through alumnae chapters in their city, because sisterhood is for life.

Every chapter sets its own timeline and requirements. The real rules live at deltasigmatheta.org.

Our Promise

No Hazing. Ever.

Delta has a strict zero-hazing rule - and so does D9Legacy. Nobody should ever be hurt, embarrassed, or bullied to join anything. If someone tries that, they are NOT being a real soror, and you should tell a trusted adult right away. Real sorors are chosen for their character, their grades, and their heart - nothing else.
