Membership Pathways

Membership Pathways

Alpha Kappa Alpha gives its members a place to grow - as students, as leaders, and as friends - while they work together for the Sorority’s big purpose: Service to All Mankind.

Real membership is by invitation only. Future members must meet high standards and finish the official Membership Intake Process (MIP). Nobody is ever allowed to make candidates do mean or embarrassing things - that breaks the Sorority’s rules and is never tolerated.

The Rules Are the Rules

Our Standards of Sisterhood

The Sorority will not invite anyone who takes part in activities outside the official Membership Intake Process, anyone who joins in mean or degrading activities, or anyone who turns in fake papers. AKA does not pick members based on the color of their skin, where their family comes from, or what they believe - and it does not allow its chapters to do that either.

No hazing. Ever. Nobody should ever be hurt, embarrassed, or bullied to join anything. If someone tries that, they are NOT being a real soror - tell a trusted adult right away. Real sorors are chosen for their character, their grades, and their heart.

Two Roads In

On a College Campus & Beyond

On a College Campus

Full-time students at a four-year college can be considered through an undergraduate chapter. Chapters share membership news through official campus notices, like the Office of Greek Life. Students should know their school’s safety rules and the Sorority’s, so the whole experience stays safe and respectful.

Graduate Chapter

Women who have finished a bachelor’s degree - or an even higher degree - can be considered through a graduate chapter in their city. Graduate membership starts with an official written letter of invitation from the chapter, and every candidate should read the Sorority’s Anti-Hazing policies first.

Requirements & Process

Application Requirements and Process

Joining AKA has different paths depending on where you are in life. Undergraduates and graduates follow different steps, and legacy applicants still have to meet the same high standards as everyone else.

Undergraduate Membership

Requirements

  • Be a full-time undergraduate student at the college where the chapter is (or a partner school of a city chapter).
  • Finish at least one full-time grading period at that school right before applying.
  • Hold a C+ average - that is a 2.50 on a 4.0 scale - and some chapters ask for even higher.
  • Attend the official Rush.

Process

An official Rush begins the undergraduate membership process, and it is announced at least two weeks ahead. Anyone who skips Rush cannot be considered that time. The Membership Intake Process begins when a candidate receives a Letter of Acceptance - and there are no membership activities between Rush and that letter. Pledging is never part of membership, before, during, or after initiation, and anyone who pledges is denied.

Graduate Membership

Requirements

  • Finish a bachelor’s degree with at least a C+ average, or earn an even higher degree.
  • Bring the motivation and talents to help the Sorority’s programs grow.
  • Attend an Information Session with the documents named in the Letter of Invitation.

Process

Graduate membership is by invitation only - chapters do not chase recruits, and candidates are asked not to chase the chapter. Graduate chapters host public events like forums and cultural programs so people can meet members and see their service work. The process begins with an official Letter of Invitation signed by the chapter president and membership chairman. Just like undergraduates: no activities before the letter, and pledging is never part of membership.

Legacy Application

A legacy candidate is the daughter, granddaughter, adopted daughter, stepdaughter, or legal ward of an AKA member. Legacies are not guaranteed a spot - they apply like everyone else. When the family member is living, she must have been active in the Sorority for at least four years in a row (48 months!) right before the application goes in.
