The Real Path

The Real Way In

Go to College

Undergraduate chapters recruit at colleges and universities. Step one for every future AKA: aim for college and shine once you’re there.

Keep Your Grades Up

Chapters look for strong students - the founders were honor students, and the standard stuck.

Serve Your Community

“Service to all mankind” isn’t a slogan, it’s a requirement. Volunteer, help out, and mean it.

Get to Know the Chapter

Attend the chapter’s public events, be yourself, and let the sorors see your character.

The Membership Process

When a chapter opens membership intake, candidates are selected for scholarship, character and service. It is always dignified - hazing is forbidden.

Graduate Chapters

After college, women join graduate chapters in their cities and keep serving for life.

Every chapter sets its own timeline and requirements. The real rules live at aka1908.com.

Our Promise

No Hazing. Ever.

AKA has a strict zero-hazing rule - and so does D9Legacy. Nobody should ever be hurt, embarrassed, or bullied to join anything. If someone tries that, they are NOT being a real soror, and you should tell a trusted adult right away. Real sorors are chosen for their character, their grades, and their heart - nothing else.
