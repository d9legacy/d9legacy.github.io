About AKA

Who We Are

The first - founded in 1908 by nine young women at Howard University who started a legacy of pink and green.
Our Name

“By Culture and By Merit”

Alpha Kappa Alpha’s motto is “By Culture and By Merit.” Merit means you earn things by doing excellent work - no cutting corners. Culture means carrying yourself with grace and lifting up the art, music and ideas around you. Put them together and you get an AKA: excellent AND elegant.

What AKA Stands For

What Every soror Lives By

Sisterhood

A bond for life. AKAs call it being “sisters” and they mean it - through college, careers and everything after.

Scholarship

The first AKA founders were honor students. Loving books and learning is baked into the pink and green.

Service to All Mankind

The famous AKA promise: help EVERYBODY - every neighborhood, every country, every kind of person.

Culture & Merit

Earn everything with excellent work, and bring beauty and good manners everywhere you go.

Quick Facts

AKA at a Glance

  • FoundedJanuary 15, 1908 at Howard University in Washington, D.C.
  • The FirstThe first Greek-letter sorority founded by African American women - the original!
  • ColorsSalmon pink and apple green - the prettiest combo in the Divine Nine.
  • SymbolsThe ivy leaf (it climbs and endures) and the pink tea rose.
  • NicknamesMembers are called AKAs or sorors. Their greeting: “skee-wee!”
  • FamilyOne of the Divine Nine, the nine historic Black Greek-letter organizations. See them all at D9Legacy.
  • SizeOver 360,000 members in more than 1,000 chapters across America and around the world.
Our Mission

Why AKA Exists

Alpha Kappa Alpha exists for one big reason: “Service to All Mankind.” Not some people - ALL people. AKAs run programs in five big areas: education, health, family, economic strength, and the arts, plus global projects that help villages and schools far from home. In the D9Legacy universe, our chapter follows the same star: be excellent, be kind, be useful.
