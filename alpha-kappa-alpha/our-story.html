How It All Started

The Leadership Change: Josh, Ivory & Jaylee D. Strong
Our Chapter’s Own Story

Two Organizations, Two Leaders

Every chapter has a beginning, and ours begins with a leadership change. This is the story of how our D9Legacy AKA chapter found its founder, Jaylee D. Strong - told the way it really happened in our universe.

The Story

The Leadership Change

The First Change

Josh Passes the Keys to Ivory

It all started when Josh decided it was time for a change in AKA. After working on building the organization, Josh decided to give Ivory the owner position. Ivory was excited about the opportunity and ready to take on the responsibility of leading AKA.

A Different Turn

Jaylee D. Strong Takes Over AKA

However, things eventually took a different turn. Josh decided to make another leadership change and officially passed the AKA owner position to Jaylee D. Strong. Jaylee was now responsible for leading AKA and helping the organization grow.

Nobody Left Out

A New Door Opens for Ivory

But Josh didn’t want Ivory to feel like she was being left out. He had another opportunity in mind for her. Josh reached out to Ivory and offered her a new position: Owner of Sigma Gamma Rho.

Josh asked Ivory if she would accept the offer and take responsibility for leading Sigma Gamma Rho. This was a completely new opportunity for Ivory, giving her the chance to build and lead an organization of her own.

A New Chapter

Two Organizations, Two Leaders

After everything, the leadership changed. Jaylee D. Strong took over AKA, while Ivory was offered the opportunity to become the owner of Sigma Gamma Rho.

Two organizations, two leaders, and a new chapter in Greek history.
