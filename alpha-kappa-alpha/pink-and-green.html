Pink & Green

The Story of the Colors

Why salmon pink and apple green mean so much - and what they say about the sisterhood.
The Colors

Chosen in 1908

When the founders started Alpha Kappa Alpha in 1908, they needed colors that felt like them. Ethel Hedgeman Lyle and Beulah Elizabeth Burke are the two founders credited with picking salmon pink and apple green - soft, joyful and full of life. More than 118 years later, those two colors are recognized all over the world.

What They Mean

More Than Pretty

Salmon Pink

Grace and sisterly love

Sorors and fans often say pink stands for grace, kindness and the love between sisters - the warm side of the sisterhood. It is the color of welcome.

Apple Green

Life and growing things

Green stands for life, hope and growth - like the ivy leaf that climbs higher every year and never lets go. It is the color of becoming who you are meant to be.

Together

Soft AND strong

Pink and green together say: you can be gentle and powerful at the same time. Pretty and smart. Kind and unstoppable. That is the AKA way.

The Tradition

Pearls Go With Everything

AKA women are also famous for wearing pearls with their pink and green. A pearl starts as a tiny grain inside an oyster and slowly becomes something beautiful - which is exactly how the sorority thinks about growing its members. Slow, patient, beautiful work.

At Magnolia Crown

Pink & Green on Campus

In the D9Legacy universe, our AKA chapter flies pink and green all over Magnolia Crown University - and nobody wears them prouder than Ahriyah “Pink Ivy” Moore, the very first member accepted into our chapter (letter dated 11/15/26), with Ava Owens right beside her in the Alpha Rho chapter, and Jaylee leading it all as chapter owner. When you see pink and green on campus, you know the sisters are up to something good.
