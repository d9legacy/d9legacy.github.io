Service to All Mankind

Helping Is the Whole Point

Alpha Kappa Alpha's purpose can be summed up in its famous service goal: “Service to All Mankind.” Not service to some people. Not service when it is easy. ALL mankind. From 1908 to today, that has meant clinics, scholarships, reading programs, disaster help and fighting for what is right. Here is what that looks like - the real history and our own chapter's kid-sized version.

The Real Legacy

A History of Helping

1935 - 1942

The Mississippi Health Project

AKA doctors and nurses, led by Dr. Dorothy Boulding Ferebee, drove deep into the Mississippi Delta to give free health care to poor farming families that nobody else would treat. One of the greatest service projects any sorority has ever run - and it happened right here in Mississippi.

1950s - 1960s

Standing Up for Civil Rights

AKA women helped power the Civil Rights Movement. Rosa Parks and Coretta Scott King were both AKAs, and chapters raised money and marched for equal rights all across America.

Every Era

Scholarships and Schools

From the beginning, AKA has given scholarships and supported education - helping students who could not afford school get through it. Our chapter keeps that tradition with its own scholarship page.

Today

A Worldwide Agenda

The real sorority still runs huge national service programs - foster care support, health, family strength, money smarts and more. See our Programs page for the current 2026-2030 agenda.

Our Chapter's Initiatives

Reading Buddies

Led by Ahriyah “Pink Ivy” Moore

Older members pair up with younger kids at Magnolia Crown to read together every week. Books plus buddies - the two best things.

Pink & Green Clean-Up Day

Led by Jaylee

Once a month the chapter picks up the campus and the neighborhood, wearing pink and green while they work. Service you can see from space (almost).

Kindness Letters

Led by Ava

The chapter writes encouraging notes for kids having a rough week. A few kind words can change somebody's whole day - that is service too.

Book Drive for Kids

The whole chapter

Every semester the sisters collect gently used books for kids who need them. Because a kid with a book is a kid with a door to anywhere.

Your Turn

Service Starts Small

You do not need to be a grown-up or a member to serve. Help one person this week and you are already living the pink and green way. Our initiatives are part of the fan-made D9Legacy universe - the real sorority's service programs are at aka1908.com.
