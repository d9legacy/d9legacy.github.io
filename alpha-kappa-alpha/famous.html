Legends in Pink & Green

Legends

Names You Should Know

&

Kamala Harris

Vice President of the United States

The first woman, first Black American, and first South Asian American Vice President - and a proud AKA from Howard University.

&

Toni Morrison

Nobel Prize-Winning Author

Wrote “Beloved” and won the Nobel Prize in Literature - one of the greatest writers who ever lived.

&

Maya Angelou

Poet & Memoirist (Honorary)

Author of “I Know Why the Caged Bird Sings.” Her words taught the world to rise.

&

Phylicia Rashad

Legendary Actress

America’s favorite TV mom from “The Cosby Show” and a Tony Award-winning stage star - also a Howard grad.

&

Mae Jemison

Astronaut

The first Black woman in space. She literally left the planet in the name of science.

&

Coretta Scott King

Civil Rights Leader (Honorary)

Carried on Dr. King’s dream and built the King Center in Atlanta.

&

Rosa Parks

Mother of the Movement (Honorary)

Her brave “no” on a Montgomery bus helped start the civil rights movement.

&

Alicia Keys

Music Superstar (Honorary)

A 15-time Grammy winner whose songs about love and strength play all over the world.

&

Jada Pinkett Smith

Actress & Producer (Honorary)

A Hollywood star and businesswoman who built her own entertainment company.

Plus thousands of AKA doctors, teachers, judges, scientists and entrepreneurs making “service to all mankind” real every day.
