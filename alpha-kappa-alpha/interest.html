Your Interest Starts Here

This page is for the D9Legacy Alpha Rho Chapter at our made-up Magnolia Crown University. It is a kid-friendly fan club, not membership in the real Alpha Kappa Alpha sorority.

You do not have to know everything yet. Read our history, meet the founders, and explore service. Think about how you can be a kind sister and a good teammate.

  1. Learn first. The form below has five quick questions about Alpha Kappa Alpha - everything you need is on this site.
  2. Tell your own story. Use your own answers and finish the interest sheet. Being honest matters more than sounding fancy.
  3. Review before you send. Check your answers, add your signature and date, and press Send my letter.
