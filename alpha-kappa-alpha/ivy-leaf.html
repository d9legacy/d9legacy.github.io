The Ivy Leaf

The Leaf That Keeps Climbing

The symbol, the magazine, and our chapter's own ivy wall.
The Symbol

Why the Ivy?

The ivy leaf is the official symbol of Alpha Kappa Alpha. Think about how ivy grows: it holds on tight, climbs through every season, survives storms and cold winters, and comes back stronger every spring. That is sisterhood. Once you grab on, you do not let go - and together you climb higher than any one person could alone.

The sorority's motto goes with it: “By Culture and By Merit.” In kid words: carry yourself with class, and earn everything you get.

The Magazine

A Leaf You Can Read

The Ivy Leaf is not just a symbol - it is also the name of the real sorority's official magazine, first published way back in 1921. For more than 100 years, The Ivy Leaf magazine has carried AKA news, history and celebration to chapters everywhere. A leaf that tells stories.

Ivy only grows up and out. AKAs say that is the plan for life: keep climbing, keep reaching, and pull somebody up behind you.

Evergreen

Ivy stays green in every season - even winter. The sisterhood stays strong in good times and hard times the same way.
