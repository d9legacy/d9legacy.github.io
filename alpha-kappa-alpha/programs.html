Programs that strengthen whole families - parents, kids and grandparents together.

Health Awareness & Well-Being

Health fairs, wellness walks and real talk about taking care of body and mind.

Money smarts: saving, credit, home buying and supporting Black-owned businesses.

Environmental & Sustainability Action

Caring for the planet - clean-ups, gardens and green habits in every community.

Celebrating Black art, music, theater and literature - culture is in the motto.

The Global Community

Serving beyond America’s shores - schools, shoes and support worldwide.

Special support for college chapters - the youngest sorors get a big boost.
