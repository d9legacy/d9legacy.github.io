The History of Iota

The Builders

Twelve men at Morgan State founded the youngest of the Divine Nine in 1963.
The Beginning

Twelve Builders in Baltimore

On September 19, 1963 - right in the heart of the civil rights movement - twelve men at Morgan State University in Baltimore founded Iota Phi Theta. America was changing fast, and these twelve wanted a new kind of brotherhood: one built on scholarship, leadership, citizenship, brotherhood and fidelity. They couldn’t rest on a long tradition - so they built one.

The Timeline

Iota Through the Years

1963

The Twelve Founders

Twelve men - Hicks, Spruill, Briscoe, Coakley, Slade, Willis, Lewis, Brown, Hudnell, Gregory, Dorsey and Williams - found Iota Phi Theta at Morgan State on September 19.

1960s - 1970s

Building Blocks

Iota spreads to new campuses across the East Coast - one brick at a time, just like the motto says.

1985

The Sweethearts

The Iota Sweethearts auxiliary forms - women who support and serve alongside the brothers.

1997

Joining the NPHC

Iota Phi Theta officially joins the National Pan-Hellenic Council - the ninth and youngest member of the Divine Nine.

2000s - 2010s

Programs Grow

The Iota Youth Alliance and Afya Njema health initiatives take root, mentoring kids and fighting for healthier Black communities.

2013 - Today

Building at 63

Iota celebrates 60+ years, with International Grand Polaris Orlando Grant taking the brothers’ story to national airwaves like the Rickey Smiley Morning Show (2026). Still building.

Why It Matters

Still Building

The youngest of the nine might have the shortest history - but the tallest ambition. Every Iota adds a brick to the tradition, and our D9Legacy chapter is building right alongside.
