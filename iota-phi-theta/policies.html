House Rules

How We Roll

No Hazing. Ever.

Iota Phi Theta has a zero-tolerance hazing policy, and the D9Legacy chapter does too. Hazing means making someone do something painful, scary, or embarrassing to join - and it is never, ever okay. If anyone treats you that way, tell a trusted adult immediately. There is also a real anti-hazing hotline anyone can call: 1-888-NOT-HAZE (1-888-668-4293).

Respect the Letters

The ΙΦΘ letters belong to the brothers who earned them. We celebrate them here, but we don't pretend to be real members, and we never use the real crest, handshake, or calls. Fans are respectful fans.

Kindness Online

In the D9Legacy universe, we treat every member like family: no teasing, no put-downs, no arguing over which org is best. Every Divine Nine organization is great - that is the whole point.

Grades First

Every real brother had to keep their grades up to join and stay. Same here: school, chores and family come before the chapter. The letters will wait for you.
