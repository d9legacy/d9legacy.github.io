Everything You Wanted to Know

What do the letters mean?

Iota Phi Theta (ΙΦΘ) - Iota is the smallest Greek letter, which fits the youngest org with the biggest ambition.

What are Iota’s colors?

Charcoal brown and gilded gold - the only brown and gold in the Divine Nine.

Why a centaur?

The centaur is Iota’s symbol - half man, half horse: strength and smarts in one body.

Is Iota really the youngest?

Yes! Founded in 1963 at Morgan State University, Iota is the ninth and youngest member of the Divine Nine - it joined the NPHC in 1997.

What does the motto mean?

“Building A Tradition, Not Resting Upon One!” - the youngest org can’t brag about 100 years of history, so it builds new history every day.

What is a Grand Polaris?

The title of Iota’s international president - the north star of the fraternity. The current one is Orlando Grant.

What is Afya Njema?

Iota’s health program - the name means “good health” in Swahili. Brothers take health seriously.

Who are the Iota Sweethearts?

Women who serve alongside the brothers - the auxiliary formed in 1985.

How old is Iota?

Founded in 1963 - the fraternity passed 60 years in 2023. Youngest of the nine!

Do Iotas only do service?

Service is the building, but brotherhood is the home: Conclaves, Founders Week, step shows and brothers for life.

Is this the real Iota Phi Theta website?

Nope! This is Josh's D9Legacy fan chapter - a kid-friendly universe built for fun and learning. The real website is iotaphitheta.org. We just love Iota here.

Can kids join?

Kids can join the D9Legacy fan chapter right now - take the quiz! The real Iota Phi Theta is for college students and adults, so school comes first.
