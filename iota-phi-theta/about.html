About Iota

Who We Are

Twelve men founded Iota in 1963 - the youngest of the Divine Nine, building its own tradition.
Our Name

“Building A Tradition, Not Resting Upon One!”

Iota Phi Theta’s motto is “Building A Tradition, Not Resting Upon One!” Founded in 1963, Iota is the youngest Divine Nine organization - so instead of bragging about a hundred-year history, the founders promised to BUILD one. Every brother adds a brick. What will you build?

The Iota Way

What Every brother Lives By

Our Mission

Why Iota Exists

Iota Phi Theta builds - leaders, communities and traditions. Founded during the civil rights movement, Iotas focus on scholarship, leadership, citizenship, brotherhood and fidelity, with programs for young people and health in Black communities. In the D9Legacy universe, the brown and gold stands for one idea: build something worth leaving behind.
