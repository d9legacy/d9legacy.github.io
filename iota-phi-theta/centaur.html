The Symbol

Why the Centaur?

Iota Phi Theta's symbol is the centaur - a creature from old Greek stories that is half man and half horse. The horse half is pure strength and speed. The man half is a thinking, feeling, deciding mind. Put them together and you get Iota's big idea: strength and wisdom in one body.

That is why Iotas are sometimes called Centaurs. A Centaur charges forward like a horse but aims like a scholar - powerful, but always pointed at something good.

Fun Facts

Centaur Did-You-Knows

An Ancient Creature

Thousands of years of stories

Centaurs galloped through Greek myths thousands of years before Iota was founded. The founders picked a symbol with real mileage on it.

Many Names

Centaurs, Iotas and more

Members go by “Iotas,” “Centaurs” and even “Thetamen.” However you say it, it means a builder of the brown and gold.

Young and Mighty

Newest, not weakest

Iota is the youngest fraternity in the Divine Nine - founded in 1963. The centaur proves young does not mean small. It means fast.

Our Chapter's Charge

Strength, Aimed at Good

In our D9Legacy chapter at Magnolia Crown, being a Centaur means one rule above all: use your strength to help, never to hurt. Strong legs for carrying books for a friend. A strong voice for standing up for somebody. That is the centaur way - and it is the D9Legacy way too.
