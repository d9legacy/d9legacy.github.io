The Story of the Colors

Earth and treasure - the colors of the builders.
The Colors

Since 1963

Iota Phi Theta's official colors are charcoal brown and gilded gold. “Gilded” is a fancy word for gold that has been polished until it glows - and that is the whole pairing: the deep brown of the earth, and gold that shines from being worked on. Perfect colors for a fraternity whose motto is “Building a Tradition, Not Resting Upon One!”

What They Mean

More Than Handsome

Charcoal Brown

Strength and steadiness

Iotas and fans say brown stands for the earth - steady, strong and dependable. Builders need solid ground, and charcoal brown is solid ground you can wear.

Gilded Gold

Achievement polished bright

Gold stands for achievement and honor - and gilded gold means it has been polished by work. Iota gold is not found, it is earned and shined.

Fun Facts

Brown & Gold Did-You-Knows

The Newest Colors

Youngest of the nine

Iota was founded in 1963, making it the youngest fraternity in the Divine Nine - so charcoal brown and gilded gold are the newest colors on this whole site.

The Yellow Rose

Iota's flower

The fraternity's flower is the yellow rose - a bloom that stands for friendship and joy. Builders with a soft side. We love that.

The Centaur Connection

Strength meets wisdom

Iota's symbol is the centaur - half man, half horse, all strength. Brown for the powerful body, gold for the brilliant mind.
