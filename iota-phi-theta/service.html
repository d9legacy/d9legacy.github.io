Building a Tradition

Helping Is the Whole Point

Iota Phi Theta was born in 1963, in the middle of the Civil Rights Movement, so service was never extra credit for Iotas - it was the reason for starting. The motto is “Building a Tradition, Not Resting Upon One!” and a lot of what they build is help for other people. Here is what that looks like - the real history and our chapter's own kid-sized version.

The Real Legacy

A History of Helping

1963

Born in the Movement

Iota was founded just weeks after the March on Washington, by twelve students who believed brotherhood should DO something. From day one, Iota men organized for their community in Baltimore and beyond.

Growing Years

The National Iota Foundation

The fraternity built its own foundation to fund scholarships and community programs - turning dues and donations into real help for real families.

Today

Youth, Health and Fatherhood

The real fraternity runs the I.O.T.A. Youth Alliance (mentoring kids and teens), the Afya Njema health program, and the Developing Better Fatherhood Project - building stronger kids, healthier families and better dads.

At Magnolia Crown

Our Chapter's Initiatives

Builder Buddies

Mentoring, Iota style

Chapter members pair up with younger kids at Magnolia Crown as “builder buddies” - reading together, playing together, and building big goals out of small weeks.

Brown & Gold Clean-Up

Service you can see

Once a month the chapter picks up the campus and the neighborhood in brown and gold. A tradition gets built one clean street at a time.

Kindness Construction Crew

Good deeds on purpose

Members pick one kind deed a week - carrying books, sitting with somebody new at lunch, helping a teacher - and report back at chapter meetings. Kindness, built like a building.
