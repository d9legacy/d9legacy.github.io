The Founders of Kappa

The Ten Founders

Ten Indiana University students who founded Kappa on January 5, 1911.
The Dreamers

Meet the Ten

&

Elder Watson Diggs

Founder - “The Dreamer”

The founder of founders: he dreamed up the fraternity, led it first, and gave Kappa its soul.

&

Ezra D. Alexander

Founder

A doctor-to-be and steady hand - he served as the young fraternity’s first secretary.

&

Byron Kenneth Armstrong

Founder

A scholar who studied at Oxford and Columbia - brains on an international level.

&

Henry Tourner Asher

Founder

A teacher and principal who spent his life educating young people.

&

Marcus Peter Blakemore

Founder

A minister and teacher - he gave Kappa much of its early spirit.

&

Paul Waymond Caine

Founder

A chef and businessman - the brother who kept everyone fed and friendly.

&

George Wesley Edmonds

Founder

A teacher who helped guide the fraternity’s first steps in Bloomington.

&

Guy Levis Grant

Founder

A pharmacist and businessman who carried Kappa’s banner his whole life.

&

Edward Giles Irvin

Founder

The youngest founder - a reporter and editor who told the fraternity’s story.

&

John Milton Lee

Founder

A soldier and scholar - remembered as one of Kappa’s earliest achievers.

All ten were students at Indiana University in Bloomington. The fraternity was first named Kappa Alpha Nu - it became Kappa Alpha Psi in 1915.
