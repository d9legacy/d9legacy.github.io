Our Chapter's Own Story

How It All Started

The Beginning

Kappa Rho Sigma

It all started when Christopher Smith decided to create his own Greek organization called Kappa Rho Sigma. Christopher was excited about having a group of his own and wanted to build something that people could become part of.

The Potential

Josh Sees a Leader

After seeing Christopher’s interest and leadership potential, Josh decided he had a bigger opportunity in mind for him. Josh believed Christopher could take on more responsibility than simply running a fictional fraternity he created himself.

The Offer

A New Chapter Opens

Josh approached Christopher with an important offer. Instead of continuing to lead Kappa Rho Sigma, Josh offered him the opportunity to become the Owner of Kappa Alpha Psi.

This was a major opportunity for Christopher. He would now have the chance to lead a much bigger organization, take on new responsibilities, and help build its future.

The Next Step

A New Level of Leadership

Christopher’s journey was changing, and a new chapter was beginning.

From creating Kappa Rho Sigma to leading Kappa Alpha Psi, Christopher Smith was ready for a new level of leadership.
