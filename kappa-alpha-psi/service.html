Achievement for Everybody

Helping Is the Whole Point

Kappa Alpha Psi's motto is “Achievement in Every Field of Human Endeavor.” But Kappas will tell you achievement is not just about trophies for yourself - it means lifting other people up so they can achieve too. Here is what that has looked like, from the real history to our chapter's own kid-sized version.

The Real Legacy

A History of Helping

1922

Guide Right

Kappa's most famous service program, Guide Right, started in the 1920s to guide young people - especially young Black men - toward high school graduation, college and good careers. More than 100 years later it is still going, making it one of the oldest mentoring programs in America.

1950s - 1960s

Standing Up for Civil Rights

Kappa men marched, organized and led during the Civil Rights Movement - fighting for equal rights in schools, at lunch counters and at voting booths all across America.

Today

Mentoring Worldwide

The real fraternity still runs Guide Right, leadership camps and scholarships around the world - helping young people aim high and hit their goals, one kid at a time.

Our Chapter's Initiatives

Crimson Crew Tutoring

Homework help, Kappa style

Chapter members pair up with younger kids at Magnolia Crown for homework help every week. Nobody fails a spelling test on our watch.

Crimson & Cream Clean-Up

Service you can see

Once a month the chapter picks up the campus and the neighborhood, wearing crimson and cream while they work. Achievement looks good on everybody.

Little Guides Day

Our own Guide Right

Once a semester, members spend a whole day helping younger students set goals - what they want to learn, who they want to be, and how to get there. Guide Right, kid-sized.
