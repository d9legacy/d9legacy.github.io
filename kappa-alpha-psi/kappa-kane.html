The Kappa Kane

The Cane With the Moves

The most famous walking stick in the Divine Nine - and what it really stands for.
The Tradition

Why the Cane?

Ask anybody about Kappa Alpha Psi and they will mention the Kappa Kane. Kappas are famous across the whole Divine Nine for twirling canes in step shows - spinning them, tapping them and dancing with them in perfect rhythm, all dressed in crimson and cream. It is one of the coolest sights in all of Black Greek life.

Fans say the cane stands for style, rhythm and confidence - a Kappa walks tall and moves with purpose. And the real secret? All that twirling takes practice. Hours and hours of it. The cane is really a trophy for hard work.

Fun Facts

Kane Did-You-Knows

Step Show Stars

Rhythm in crimson

Kappa step teams with their canes are legends of the step show - the kind of performance that makes a whole gymnasium go quiet and then explode.

The Kappa Shimmy

A signature move

Kappas are also known for the “Kappa shimmy” - a smooth little shoulder shake that brothers do when they are feeling the moment. If you see it, you know.

The Diamond

Kappa's jewel

The fraternity's jewel is the diamond - precious, bright and formed under pressure. Just like a Kappa man.

Our Chapter's Rule

Canes Are for Stepping

In our D9Legacy chapter at Magnolia Crown, canes are for stepping, twirling and celebrating - never for hitting or horseplay. A Kappa Kane in kind hands is a work of art. That is the whole tradition.
