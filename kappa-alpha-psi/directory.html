Membership Requirements

For our D9Legacy fan club only. These steps summarize this chapter's interest sheet and rule book.

  1. Learn about your chapterExplore the history, founders and service pages. You do not have to know everything before you begin.
  2. Complete both partsTake this chapter's quiz and fill out its full interest sheet. Use your own honest answers, then add your signature and date.
  3. Be kind and respectfulBe a good teammate. No bullying, teasing, hazing or leaving people out.
  4. Put school and service firstGive school your best effort and share how you can help others at home, at school or in your community.
  5. Follow the chapter rulesRead the full interest rule book before sending. Send one sheet per person and keep private information out of public spaces.
  6. Wait for a chapter decisionFinishing a quiz or sheet is not automatic acceptance. Follow the chapter's review and acceptance-letter steps.

Practice-site note: forms, letters and inboxes are saved in the browser on the device being used. Sending a sheet does not deliver it to the owner on another phone or iPad.
