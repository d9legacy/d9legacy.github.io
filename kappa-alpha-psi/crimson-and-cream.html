Crimson & Cream

The Story of the Colors

Why crimson and cream mean so much - and what they say about the brotherhood.
The Colors

Since 1911

From the very beginning at Indiana University, Kappa Alpha Psi's colors have been crimson and cream - a deep, bold red paired with a soft, warm cream. More than 115 years later, you can spot those two colors on campuses all over the world and know exactly whose they are.

What They Mean

More Than Sharp

Crimson

Boldness and achievement

Kappas and fans say crimson stands for courage, energy and achievement - the fire to chase big goals. It matches the fraternity's motto perfectly: “Achievement in Every Field of Human Endeavor.”

Cream

Class and warmth

Cream is the softer half of the pair - smooth, classy and warm, like the brotherhood itself. Bold on the outside, kind on the inside.

Fun Facts

Crimson & Cream Did-You-Knows

Day One Colors

Older than sliced bread

Crimson and cream have been Kappa's colors since 1911 - older than TV, older than traffic lights, older than sliced bread (really!).

The Nupes

A famous nickname

Kappas are often called “Nupes,” a nickname that goes back to the fraternity's first name, Kappa Alpha Nu. If you hear somebody say “the Nupes wear crimson and cream,” now you know why.

Known Worldwide

Spot them anywhere

From Indiana to Africa, Europe and the Caribbean, crimson and cream tell you a Kappa is near. Two colors, one worldwide brotherhood.
