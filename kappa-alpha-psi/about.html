About Kappa

Who We Are

Ten students at Indiana University in 1911 founded a brotherhood obsessed with achievement.
Our Name

“Achievement in Every Field of Human Endeavor”

Kappa Alpha Psi was built on one word: achievement. The brothers greet each other with “Phi Nu Pi” and chase one goal - “Achievement in Every Field of Human Endeavor.” Every field means EVERY field: classrooms, courts, labs, stages. Pick your field, then achieve in it.

Quick Facts

Kappa at a Glance

  • FoundedJanuary 5, 1911 at Indiana University in Bloomington, Indiana.
  • The TenFounded by ten young men - led by Elder Watson Diggs, “the Dreamer.”
  • Original NameFirst called Kappa Alpha Nu - changed to Kappa Alpha Psi in 1915.
  • ColorsCrimson and cream - bold red and soft cream.
  • SymbolThe diamond. The flower is the red carnation.
  • NicknamesMembers are called Kappas or Nupes. Their greeting: Phi Nu Pi.
  • FamilyOne of the Divine Nine. See all nine at D9Legacy.
Our Mission

Why Kappa Exists

Kappa Alpha Psi exists to inspire achievement - in school, in careers, in service. Its crown jewel is Guide Right, a mentoring program running since 1922 that points young people toward college and good lives. In the D9Legacy universe, the crimson and cream stands for one thing: whatever you do, achieve in it.
