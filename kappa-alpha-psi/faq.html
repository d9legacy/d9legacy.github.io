Everything You Wanted to Know

What do the letters mean?

Kappa Alpha Psi (ΚΑΨ). The fraternity started as Kappa Alpha Nu in 1911 and became Kappa Alpha Psi in 1915.

What are Kappa’s colors?

Crimson and cream - bold and classy. (Yes, Delta shares crimson and cream - but only one of them has the diamond.)

What does Phi Nu Pi mean?

It’s the brothers’ famous greeting! What it stands for is brothers-only knowledge - fans just enjoy hearing it.

Why is Kappa’s symbol the diamond?

Diamonds form under pressure and come out shining - like the founders, who built greatness at a school that didn’t always welcome them.

Who was the Dreamer?

Elder Watson Diggs - the lead founder who imagined the whole fraternity and made it happen.

What is Guide Right?

Kappa’s mentoring program for young people, running since 1922 - one of the oldest in America.

What is a Grand Polemarch?

That’s the title of Kappa’s national president! The current one is Jimmy McMikle, the 35th.

Was Arthur Ashe a Kappa?

Yes - the first Black man to win Wimbledon was a proud Nupe. So was Wilt Chamberlain, who scored 100 points in one game.

What is a Nupe?

The nickname for members of Kappa Alpha Psi - from the “Nu” in the fraternity’s original name.

Do Kappas only do service?

Service is the work, but brotherhood is the fun: Conclaves, Founders Day, the famous cane strolls, and brothers for life.

Is this the real Kappa Alpha Psi website?

Nope! This is Josh's D9Legacy fan chapter - a kid-friendly universe built for fun and learning. The real website is kappaalphapsi1911.com. We just love Kappa here.

Can kids join?

Kids can join the D9Legacy fan chapter right now - take the quiz! The real Kappa Alpha Psi is for college students and adults, so school comes first.
