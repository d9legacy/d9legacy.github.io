This page is for the D9Legacy Sigma Rho Chapter at our made-up Magnolia Crown University. It is a kid-friendly fan club, not membership in the real Sigma Gamma Rho sorority.

You do not have to know everything yet. Read our history, meet the founders, and explore service. Think about how you can be a kind sister and a good teammate.

  1. Learn first. The form below has five quick questions about Sigma Gamma Rho - everything you need is on this site.

  3. Review before you send. Check your answers, add your signature and date, and press Send my letter.
