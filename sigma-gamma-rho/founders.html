The Founders of Sigma

The Seven Founders

Seven schoolteachers at Butler University who founded Sigma on November 12, 1922.
The Educators

Meet the Seven

&

Mary Lou Allison Gardner Little

Founder

The sorority’s first Grand Basileus (that’s president) - a teacher who led the sisterhood’s first steps.

&

Dorothy Hanley Whiteside

Founder

A teacher and organizer who helped the young sorority grow beyond Indianapolis.

&

Vivian Irene White Marbury

Founder

A teacher whose energy helped turn seven friends into a national sisterhood.

&

Nannie Mae Gahn Johnson

Founder

An educator who brought warmth and wisdom to the founding seven.

&

Hattie Mae Annette Dulin Redford

Founder

A teacher and the sorority’s first Grand Epistoleus - the sister who kept the records.

&

Bessie Mae Downey Rhoades Martin

Founder

The youngest founder - a teacher who helped plan the sorority’s earliest days.

&

Cubena McClure

Founder

A gifted teacher who designed the sorority’s first pin - gone too soon, never forgotten.

All seven were teachers. Sigma Gamma Rho is the only Divine Nine sorority founded at a predominantly white university - Butler University, Indianapolis, 1922.
