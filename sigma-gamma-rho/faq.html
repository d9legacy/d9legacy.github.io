Everything You Wanted to Know

What do the letters mean?

Sigma Gamma Rho (ΣΓΡ) - often shortened to SGRho, which fans say “ess-jee-ro.”

What are Sigma’s colors?

Royal blue and gold - royal because these teachers run a classy kingdom.

Why a poodle?

The French toy poodle is Sigma’s mascot - smart, loyal, and always put-together. The flower is the yellow tea rose.

Was Sigma really founded by teachers?

Yes! All seven founders were schoolteachers - education has been the heart of the sorority since November 12, 1922.

What makes Sigma’s founding unique?

It’s the only Divine Nine sorority founded at a predominantly white university - Butler University in Indianapolis.

What is Swim 1922?

Sigma’s partnership with USA Swimming that teaches swimming and water safety - named for the founding year and built to save lives.

What is a Grand Basileus?

That’s the title of Sigma’s international president! The current one is Marica T. Harris, the 26th.

Who can be a Rhoer or Rhosebud?

They’re Sigma’s youth clubs - Rhosebuds for little girls, Rhoers for teens, Philos for adult friends of the sorority.

How old is Sigma?

Founded in 1922 - the sorority celebrated its 100th birthday in 2022.

Do Sigmas only do service?

Service is the motto, but sisterhood is the fun: Boules, Founders Day, step shows and friends for life.

Is this the real Sigma Gamma Rho website?

Nope! This is Josh's D9Legacy fan chapter - a kid-friendly universe built for fun and learning. The real website is sgrho1922.org. We just love Sigma here.

Can kids join?

Kids can join the D9Legacy fan chapter right now - take the quiz! The real Sigma Gamma Rho is for college students and adults, so school comes first.
