Famous Sigmas

Legends in Royal Blue & Gold

Oscar winners, music legends and TV stars - Sigmas shine bright.
Legends

Names You Should Know

&

Hattie McDaniel

Oscar-Winning Actress

The first African American to win an Academy Award (1940, “Gone with the Wind”). She opened Hollywood’s door.

&

Marilyn McCoo

Grammy-Winning Singer

Lead voice of The 5th Dimension - “Aquarius,” “Up, Up and Away” - and a TV star for decades.

&

MC Lyte

Hip-Hop Pioneer

One of rap’s first female superstars - she proved women could rock the mic with the best.

&

Victoria Rowell

Actress & Author

Star of “The Young and the Restless” and “Diagnosis: Murder,” and a champion for foster kids.

&

Kelly Price

R&B Star

A powerhouse singer and songwriter whose voice fills churches and arenas alike.

&

Vanessa Bell Armstrong

Gospel Legend

One of gospel music’s greatest voices - a voice that lifts whole rooms.

&

Desreta Jackson

Actress & Producer

Known from “The Color Purple” - now making films and haircare products of her own.

&

Tonya Edwards

WNBA Star & Coach

An NCAA champion and pro player who became a college head coach - teaching on the court.

&

Wynona Lipman

State Senator

The first Black woman elected to the New Jersey Senate - a Sigma in the statehouse.

Plus generations of Sigma teachers, principals, nurses and organizers - the founders’ students’ students, still teaching.
