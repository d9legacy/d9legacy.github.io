About Sigma

Who We Are

Seven schoolteachers founded Sigma in 1922 - and they’ve been teaching the world to serve ever since.
Our Name

“Greater Service, Greater Progress”

Sigma Gamma Rho’s motto is “Greater Service, Greater Progress.” It works like a seesaw: when service goes up, progress goes up. Want a better school, a better neighborhood, a better world? Serve more. That’s the whole formula - seven schoolteachers figured it out in 1922.

What Sigma Stands For

What Every soror Lives By

Sisterhood

Seven teachers started as friends and stayed sisters for life. That’s the Sigma bond.

Scholarship

Founded by educators - learning isn’t a hobby here, it’s the family business.

Service

“Greater service” is in the motto. Sigmas show up for their communities, always.

Progress

The second half of the motto: serving isn’t enough unless it moves people forward.

Quick Facts

Sigma at a Glance

  • FoundedNovember 12, 1922 at Butler University in Indianapolis, Indiana.
  • The SevenFounded by seven young schoolteachers - educators from day one.
  • One of a KindThe only Divine Nine sorority founded at a predominantly white university.
  • ColorsRoyal blue and gold - regal, bright, and proud.
  • SymbolThe French toy poodle - fancy, smart and loyal. The flower is the yellow tea rose.
  • NicknamesMembers are called Sigmas, sorors, or SGRhos (ess-jee-ros).
  • FamilyOne of the Divine Nine. See all nine at D9Legacy.
Our Mission

Why Sigma Exists

Sigma Gamma Rho’s mission is right in the motto: greater service, greater progress. Seven teachers founded it to make their community better, and chapters today do the same - swimming lessons that save lives, book bags full of school supplies, essay contests and wellness programs. In the D9Legacy universe, we keep it teacher-approved: learn, help, repeat.
