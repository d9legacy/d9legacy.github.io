Royal Blue & Gold

The Story of the Colors

Why royal blue and gold mean so much - and what they say about the sisterhood.
The Colors

Royal Blue and Gold

When the seven teachers organized Sigma Gamma Rho in 1922, they chose royal blue and gold - colors fit for queens. More than a century later, that blue and gold shine on campuses and in communities all over the world.

What They Mean

More Than Pretty

Royal Blue

Loyalty and truth

Sorors and fans often say royal blue stands for loyalty, truth and deep, steady sisterhood - like the sky that shows up every single day. It is the color of keeping your word.

Gold

Excellence and light

Gold stands for excellence - doing your best and letting your light shine. Gold does not hide, and neither do Sigma women when somebody needs help.

Together

Greater service, greater progress

Blue and gold together carry the sorority's famous slogan: Greater Service, Greater Progress. Be true, be excellent, and help everybody move forward. That is the Sigma way.

The Tradition

Roses, Poodles and EE-Yip

Sigma Gamma Rho's flower is the yellow tea rose - golden, of course. Its mascot is the elegant poodle, and its famous call is “EE-yip!” - a sound you will never forget once you hear it. The sorority's magazine is called The AURORA, named for the dawn, because Sigma women are always starting something bright.

At Magnolia Crown

Blue & Gold on Campus

In the D9Legacy universe, our Sigma Gamma Rho chapter flies royal blue and gold all over Magnolia Crown University. When you see that blue and gold on campus, you know the sisters are planning something greater.
