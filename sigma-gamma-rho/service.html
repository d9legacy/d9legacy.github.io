Greater Service, Greater Progress

Serving Is the Slogan

Sigma Gamma Rho's slogan is “Greater Service, Greater Progress.” It means: the more you help, the further everybody goes. From 1922 to today, Sigma women have run health programs, book drives, swim lessons and youth clubs - all to lift families up. Here is the real history, plus our chapter's kid-sized version.

The Real Legacy

A History of Helping

1922

Seven Teachers Get to Work

The founders were schoolteachers - service was already their day job. They built the sorority in Indianapolis to do even more good, together.

1929

Made Official

On December 30, 1929, Sigma Gamma Rho became an incorporated national sorority, with its Alpha chapter at Butler University. Official paperwork, official service.

Every Era

Books and Health

Two of the sorority's most loved programs: Operation BigBookBag, which stuffs backpacks with books and school supplies for kids, and national health projects like Project Reassurance and cancer awareness work named for Hattie McDaniel.

Today

Swim 1922

With USA Swimming, Sigma Gamma Rho runs Swim 1922, teaching kids and families water safety and how to swim - a program that literally saves lives.

At Magnolia Crown

Our Chapter's Initiatives

Big Book Bag Day

The whole chapter

Just like the real Operation BigBookBag, our chapter collects books and school supplies for kids who need them. A full backpack is a full head start.

Water Safety Week

Inspired by Swim 1922

The sisters teach simple pool rules and swim safety to younger kids at Magnolia Crown. Be safe first, then make a splash.

Golden Compliment Jar

Every sister

A jar full of kind notes for classmates having a tough week. Pull one out, hand it over, watch a day get better.

Blue & Gold Clean-Up

The whole chapter

Once a month the chapter tidies the campus and the neighborhood, wearing royal blue and gold while they work.

Your Turn

Service Starts Small

You do not need to be grown to serve. Help one person this week and you are already living greater service, greater progress. Our initiatives are part of the fan-made D9Legacy universe - the real sorority's programs are at sgrho1922.org.
