Sigma at Work

Swim 1922

With USA Swimming, Sigmas teach kids and families to swim - saving lives one lesson at a time. Named for the founding year.

Backpacks stuffed with school supplies for kids who need them. Teachers started this sorority, remember?

Project Reassurance

Teaching families about healthy choices, healthy babies and strong communities.

Mwanamugimu Essay Contest

A writing contest that gets students thinking and writing about big ideas. Sharpen those pencils.

Women’s Wellness Initiative

Health education and support for women and families - body and mind.

Youth Mentoring

Rhoers (girls), Philos (friends) and Rhosebuds (little ones) - Sigma’s clubs for young people to grow up served and serving.

These mirror the real sorority’s national programs. Our D9Legacy chapter runs kid-sized versions at Magnolia Crown University.
