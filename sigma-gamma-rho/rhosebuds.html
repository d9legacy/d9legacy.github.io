For Kids and Teens

The Sisterhood Starts Young

The real Sigma Gamma Rho runs official affiliate clubs so girls and women who are not sorority members can still be part of the family - learning, serving and leading right alongside the sisters. Here is the whole crew, from littlest to biggest.

The Affiliates

Meet the Family

Rhosebuds

The youngest girls

Rhosebuds are the littlest members of the Sigma family - young girls who learn confidence, kindness and good habits with the sisters cheering them on. Small roses, big futures.

Rhoers

Teen girls

The Rhoer Club is for teenage girls: real talk about school and life, community service projects, and leadership practice. Founder Vivian White Marbury is remembered for inspiring clubs just like this one.

Philos

Women friends of Sigma

Philo Affiliates are grown women who are friends of the sorority and serve right beside the sisters - proof you do not need letters to live the slogan.

Why It Matters

Grow a Rose Early

Seven teachers started Sigma Gamma Rho, so of course the sorority invests in kids. A Rhosebud today could be a Rhoer tomorrow and a Soror after that - or just a kind, confident person who learned young that service matters. Either way, everybody wins.
