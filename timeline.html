Alpha Phi Alpha ΑΦΑ • December 4, 1906

Cornell University, Ithaca, New York. Founded by the Seven Jewels.

Henry Arthur Callis, Charles Henry Chapman, Eugene Kinckle Jones, George Biddle Kelley, Nathaniel Allison Murray, Robert Harold Ogle, Vertner Woodson Tandy

In 1930, between Zeta Phi Beta (1920) and Iota Phi Theta (1963), the nine came together at Howard University to form the National Pan-Hellenic Council - the Divine Nine as one family.
